## How to write each card

- **Shape.** Same as the subject's existing MISCON cards (see `ITEM-0450-5.2-035-MISCON` or `ITEM-0450-4.3-015-MISCON`): the prompt quotes a learner making the confusion in a short, plausible sentence, often in a named Namibian business context, and asks the learner to explain what is wrong. Write each prompt yourself; do not reuse a learner quote from another card.
- **Answer.** A discrimination, not a definition repeated: name the error, state the boundary between the two ideas, and give the test that tells them apart, applied to the quoted sentence. `marking_guidance` must name the error (the check looks for error / mistake / confus / wrongly / in fact) and say what a 6-mark answer needs.
- **Claims.** Every card cites the claims it rests on. Use claims already in `topics/<T>/claims/canonical_claim_ledger.json` where they say what the card needs. Where the boundary itself is not yet a claim, add one, map it to the slot's objective, and teach it in a content-unit block (an extended block gets a new `authored_hash`, its old one as `prior_hash`, its `claims_seen` advanced; a new block is stamped as before).
- **No near-duplicates.** Read the topic's existing items first. Where a card already teaches the definition of one side, the MISCON card must do the discriminating work, not restate it (check C-21).

## Watch these

- **2.1 motivators vs hygiene factors**: both sides are Herzberg's. Keep to Taylor and Herzberg, which is what the syllabus names; do not bring in other theorists.
- **6.3 currency appreciation vs depreciation**: the direction and its effect on exporters and importers only. **No exchange-rate calculation** (the syllabus excludes it and C-11 scans for it). A rate quoted in words is fine; converting a price is not.
- **3.3 price vs cost** and the three pricing-method slots: no PED formula or PED calculation (excluded).
- **5.3 cash-flow forecast vs income statement**: do not construct an income statement (excluded). Describe what each records and when.
- **6.2** two cards on the same objective: one separates ethical from environmental, the other separates ethical from legal. Keep them distinct.
