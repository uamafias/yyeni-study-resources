## Content units

**CU-6118-4.2.1** — sub-topic 4.2.1, 5 objectives, 477–786 words
- `OBJ-6118-4.2.1-01` tier 2 — outline the difference between luminous and illuminated objects
- `OBJ-6118-4.2.1-02` tier 4 — explain that light travels in straight lines and how this leads to the - formation of shadows - appearance of the image (pin-hole camera) - eclipses of the sun and moon, including the terms umbra and penumbra
- `OBJ-6118-4.2.1-02-M01` tier 2 — Explain shadows and straight-line propagation
- `OBJ-6118-4.2.1-02-M02` tier 2 — Explain image formation in a pinhole camera
- `OBJ-6118-4.2.1-02-M03` tier 2 — Explain solar and lunar eclipses using umbra and penumbra

**CU-6118-4.3.1** — sub-topic 4.3.1, 4 objectives, 382–629 words
- `OBJ-6118-4.3.1-01` tier 4 — explain the use of apparatus such as a ray-box, mirror, glass window, Perspex strips, protractor and rectangular block to investigate what happens when light falls on different objects that are translucent, transparent and opaque
- `OBJ-6118-4.3.1-01-M01` tier 2 — Investigate transparent materials
- `OBJ-6118-4.3.1-01-M02` tier 2 — Investigate translucent materials
- `OBJ-6118-4.3.1-01-M03` tier 2 — Investigate opaque materials

**CU-6118-4.3.2** — sub-topic 4.3.2, 8 objectives, 764–1258 words
- `OBJ-6118-4.3.2-01` tier 2 — draw a ray diagram to show the formation of the image of a point on an object produced by a plane mirror
- `OBJ-6118-4.3.2-02` tier 4 — give the characteristics (position, nature, size and lateral inversion) of an optical image formed by a plane mirror
- `OBJ-6118-4.3.2-02-M01` tier 1 — Describe plane-mirror image position and nature
- `OBJ-6118-4.3.2-02-M02` tier 1 — Describe plane-mirror image size and lateral inversion
- `OBJ-6118-4.3.2-03` tier 1 — identify and measure the angles of incidence and of reflection (angle of incidence is equal to angle of reflection) and the normal at the point a ray strikes the surface
- `OBJ-6118-4.3.2-04` tier 2 — describe advantages and disadvantages of reflection, including everyday applications of reflection such as the uses of different kinds of mirrors and the reflecting surfaces behind lights
- `OBJ-6118-4.3.2-05` tier 2 — recall and use the law: angle of incidence = angle of reflection
- `OBJ-6118-4.3.2-06` tier 2 — perform simple constructions, measurements and calculations on a ray diagram
