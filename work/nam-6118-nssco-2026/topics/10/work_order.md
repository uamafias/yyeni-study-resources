## Content units

**CU-6118-2.10.3** — sub-topic 2.10.3, 9 objectives, 932–1536 words
- `OBJ-6118-2.10.3-01` tier 2 — give examples of energy in different forms, its conversion and conservation and apply the principle of energy conservation to simple examples (e.g. the kinetic energy changing to potential energy in a pendulum and the sum of the two is constant if air resistance is negligible)
- `OBJ-6118-2.10.3-02` tier 2 — describe energy transfer in terms of work done and make calculations involving F × d
- `OBJ-6118-2.10.3-03` tier 4 — describe kinetic energy as energy of motion and potential energy as energy of position (e.g. gravitational potential energy and strain potential energy)
- `OBJ-6118-2.10.3-03-M01` tier 2 — Describe kinetic energy
- `OBJ-6118-2.10.3-03-M02` tier 2 — Describe gravitational potential energy
- `OBJ-6118-2.10.3-03-M03` tier 2 — Describe strain potential energy
- `OBJ-6118-2.10.3-04` tier 4 — recall and use the formulae for kinetic energy and gravitational potential energy E = mgh and E = ½ mv 2 p k
- `OBJ-6118-2.10.3-04-M01` tier 2 — Use gravitational potential energy Ep = mgh
- `OBJ-6118-2.10.3-04-M02` tier 2 — Use kinetic energy Ek = ½mv²

**CU-6118-2.10.4** — sub-topic 2.10.4, 10 objectives, 1035–1707 words
- `OBJ-6118-2.10.4-01` tier 1 — define work done as the product of the magnitude of a force and distance moved in the direction of the force   
- `OBJ-6118-2.10.4-02` tier 2 — Recall and use work = force × displacement in the force direction, W = Fd.
- `OBJ-6118-2.10.4-03` tier 1 — explain that energy is the ability to do work and that the joule [J] is the unit of both work and energy
- `OBJ-6118-2.10.4-04` tier 1 — state the unit of work as 1 J = 1 N m
- `OBJ-6118-2.10.4-05` tier 4 — identify examples of levers, gears and pulleys and explain how they are used for making the effort easier (NOTE: the work stays the same but the force required becomes less)
- `OBJ-6118-2.10.4-05-M01` tier 2 — Explain how a lever trades force against distance
- `OBJ-6118-2.10.4-05-M02` tier 2 — Explain the action of gears
- `OBJ-6118-2.10.4-05-M03` tier 2 — Explain how a pulley reduces required effort
- `OBJ-6118-2.10.4-06` tier 1 — define efficiency as the percentage of useful energy output from the total energy input
- `OBJ-6118-2.10.4-07` tier 2 — Use efficiency = useful energy output / total energy input × 100%.

**CU-6118-2.10.5** — sub-topic 2.10.5, 2 objectives, 207–341 words
- `OBJ-6118-2.10.5-01` tier 1 — define power as the rate of doing work or the rate of energy conversion (1 J s -1 = 1 W)
- `OBJ-6118-2.10.5-02` tier 2 — Use power = energy transferred / time = work done / time.
