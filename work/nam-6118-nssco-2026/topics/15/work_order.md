## Content units

**CU-6118-3.3.1** — sub-topic 3.3.1, 3 objectives, 316–511 words
- `OBJ-6118-3.3.1-01` tier 1 — define conduction of heat as the flow of heat through a material without any flow of the material
- `OBJ-6118-3.3.1-02` tier 2 — describe experiments to demonstrate the properties of good and bad conductors of heat
- `OBJ-6118-3.3.1-03` tier 2 — give a simple molecular account of the heat transfer in insulators and the migration of electrons in metals

**CU-6118-3.3.2** — sub-topic 3.3.2, 4 objectives, 422–682 words
- `OBJ-6118-3.3.2-01` tier 1 — define convection as the flow of liquid or gas caused by a change in density, in which the whole medium moves and carries heat energy with it
- `OBJ-6118-3.3.2-02` tier 2 — explain the difference between conduction and convection in terms of the physical movement of the medium
- `OBJ-6118-3.3.2-03` tier 2 — describe convection in fluids as due to density change
- `OBJ-6118-3.3.2-04` tier 2 — describe experiments to illustrate convection

**CU-6118-3.3.3** — sub-topic 3.3.3, 5 objectives, 528–853 words
- `OBJ-6118-3.3.3-01` tier 1 — define radiation as the transfer of heat by electromagnetic waves which do not require a medium
- `OBJ-6118-3.3.3-02` tier 1 — identify infra-red radiation as the part of the electromagnetic spectrum
- `OBJ-6118-3.3.3-03` tier 4 — describe experiments to show the properties of good and bad emitters and good and bad absorbers of infra-red radiation
- `OBJ-6118-3.3.3-03-M01` tier 2 — Investigate good and poor infrared emitters
- `OBJ-6118-3.3.3-03-M02` tier 2 — Investigate good and poor infrared absorbers

**CU-6118-3.3.4** — sub-topic 3.3.4, 4 objectives, 422–682 words
- `OBJ-6118-3.3.4-01` tier 4 — identify and explain some of the everyday applications and consequences of conduction, convection and radiation
- `OBJ-6118-3.3.4-01-M01` tier 2 — Explain an application of conduction
- `OBJ-6118-3.3.4-01-M02` tier 2 — Explain an application of convection
- `OBJ-6118-3.3.4-01-M03` tier 2 — Explain an application of thermal radiation
