## Content units

**CU-6118-2.7** — sub-topic 2.7, 7 objectives, 1056–1654 words
- `OBJ-6118-2.7-01` tier 2 — describe the moment of a force as a measure of its turning effect and give everyday examples (such as crowbar, wheelbarrow, pliers, scissors, tweezers or tongs)
- `OBJ-6118-2.7-02` tier 2 — describe the difference between moment and work (moment is a vector, work is a scalar; and that the distance involved in moment is perpendicular to the force whilst the distance involved in work is in the same direction as the applied force)
- `OBJ-6118-2.7-03` tier 1 — identify load, effort and fulcrum in everyday examples of common machines (e.g. spanner, wheelbarrow)
- `OBJ-6118-2.7-04` tier 2 — recall and use the formula moment = force × perpendicular distance
- `OBJ-6118-2.7-05` tier 1 — state that, when there is no resultant force and no resultant turning effect, a system is in equilibrium
- `OBJ-6118-2.7-06` tier 2 — describe an experiment (involving vertical forces) to verify that there is no net moment on a body in equilibrium
- `OBJ-6118-2.7-07` tier 2 — use the conditions of equilibrium in simple calculations

**CU-6118-2.8** — sub-topic 2.8, 2 objectives, 301–472 words
- `OBJ-6118-2.8-01` tier 2 — describe an experiment to determine the position of the centre of mass of a plane lamina
- `OBJ-6118-2.8-02` tier 2 — describe qualitatively the effect of the position of the centre of mass on the stability of simple objects

**CU-6118-2.9** — sub-topic 2.9, 2 objectives, 301–472 words
- `OBJ-6118-2.9-01` tier 2 — describe the effect of friction on objects and how friction depends on the surfaces
- `OBJ-6118-2.9-02` tier 2 — describe how to reduce friction and discuss the advantages and disadvantages of friction in everyday life
