- `OBJ-6118-2.11-01` tier 1 — define pressure as the perpendicular force acting on a unit area F 
- `OBJ-6118-2.11-02` tier 2 — Use pressure = force / area, P = F/A; convert pascal and kilopascal units.

- `OBJ-6118-2.11-10` tier 2 — perform calculations involving interconversion of units of pressure (mm Hg, Pa)
- `OBJ-6118-2.11-11` tier 1 — state that a wind is caused by high and low pressure systems
