## Content units

**CU-6118-3.1** — sub-topic 3.1, 24 objectives, 2755–4445 words
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-01` tier 4 — explain the kinetic particle model of matter as: - the particles and the space the particles occupy - the forces between particles - the constant state of motion of the particles - elastic collisions between particles - an increase in kinetic energy of the particles as temperature rises
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-01-M01` tier 2 — Describe particles and the space between them
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-01-M02` tier 2 — Describe forces between particles
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-01-M03` tier 2 — Describe continual particle motion
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-01-M04` tier 2 — Describe elastic particle collisions qualitatively
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-01-M05` tier 2 — Relate temperature rise to increased mean kinetic energy
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-02` tier 4 — explain and analyse by means of the kinetic particle model of matter the processes of expansion and compressibility in solids, liquids and gases and diffusion in liquids and gases
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-02-M01` tier 2 — Explain thermal expansion in solids
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-02-M02` tier 2 — Explain thermal expansion in liquids and gases
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-02-M03` tier 2 — Explain differences in compressibility
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-02-M04` tier 2 — Explain diffusion in liquids and gases
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-03` tier 2 — identify and explain some of the everyday applications and consequences of thermal expansion
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-04` tier 4 — compare the differences between phases of matter in terms of differences in the behaviour and arrangement of particles
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-04-M01` tier 2 — Compare particle arrangements in solid, liquid and gas
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-04-M02` tier 2 — Compare particle motion and forces across phases
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-05` tier 2 — interpret the change from one phase of matter to another which involves an energy change (heat energy is either given out or taken in) and explain that the temperature of matter is constant during a phase change
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-06` tier 2 — describe the phases of matter and explain their inter-conversion in terms of the kinetic particle model in everyday life
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-07` tier 2 — interpret the temperature of a gas in terms of the motion of its molecules
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-08` tier 2 — describe qualitatively the pressure of a gas in terms of the motion of its molecules
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-09` tier 2 — describe qualitatively the effect of a change of temperature on the pressure of a gas at constant volume (Pressure Law)
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-10` tier 2 — describe qualitatively the effect of a change of temperature on the volume of a gas at constant pressure (Charles’s Law)
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-11` tier 2 — describe qualitatively the effect of a change of volume on the pressure of a gas at constant temperature (Boyle’s Law)
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-12` tier 1 — describe the random motion of particles in a suspension as evidence for the kinetic particle model of matter
- `OBJ-6118-3.1-13` tier 2 — describe this random motion (sometimes known as Brownian motion) in terms of random molecular bombardment
