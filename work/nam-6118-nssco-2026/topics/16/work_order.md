## Content units

**CU-6118-4.1** — sub-topic 4.1, 23 objectives, 2020–3440 words
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-01` tier 1 — define a pulse as a single disturbance
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-02` tier 1 — define oscillations or simple harmonic motion as any repeated to-and-fro motion of a fluid or elastic solid, e.g. a tuning-fork, pendulum or stretched string
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-03` tier 2 — describe what is meant by wave motion (propagation), an oscillation which transfers energy from one place to another without any net movement of the medium, as illustrated by vibration in ropes, springs and by experiments using water waves
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-04` tier 1 — state typical values of the speed of sound and light in air, water and glass and the speed of light in a vacuum
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-05` tier 2 — explain why sound travels faster in solids than in gases, using ideas about molecules
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-06` tier 2 — outline some of the practical consequences of the difference of the speed of sound and light in air, such as the observation that thunder always follows lightning
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-07` tier 2 — distinguish between transverse and longitudinal waves (in terms of the direction of vibration of the particles and the direction of travel of the wave) and give examples of each
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-08` tier 4 — give the meaning of speed c, frequency f, period T, wavelength  and amplitude
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-08-M01` tier 1 — Define wave speed
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-08-M02` tier 1 — Define frequency
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-08-M03` tier 1 — Define period
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-08-M04` tier 1 — Define wavelength
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-08-M05` tier 1 — Define amplitude
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-09` tier 4 — 1  recall and use the equations c = f and f = 𝑇
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-09-M01` tier 2 — Use wave speed = frequency × wavelength
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-09-M02` tier 2 — Use frequency = 1/period
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-10` tier 1 — define the term wave front in wave motion, as the line containing adjacent points that are in the same phase
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-11` tier 4 — draw circular and plane wave fronts on the surface of water
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-11-M01` tier 2 — Draw circular wavefronts
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-11-M02` tier 2 — Draw plane wavefronts
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-12` tier 4 — use the term wave front to describe the observation of water waves in a ripple tank undergoing: - reflection at a plane surface (wave fronts reflect at the same angle to the surface) - refraction due to a change in depth, changing the speed, and the wavelength but not the frequency
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-12-M01` tier 2 — Describe reflected wavefronts
- `OBJ-6118-4.1-12-M02` tier 2 — Describe refracted wavefronts when water depth changes
