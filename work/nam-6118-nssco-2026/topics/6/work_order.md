## Content units

**CU-6118-2.4** — sub-topic 2.4, 19 objectives, 2125–3415 words
- `OBJ-6118-2.4-01` tier 2 — state that a force, measured in newtons, may produce a change in size, shape or velocity of a body and relate these ideas to Newton’s first and second laws of motion
- `OBJ-6118-2.4-02` tier 1 — describe the ways in which a force may change the motion of a body
- `OBJ-6118-2.4-03` tier 4 — identify in everyday life:  different forces (e.g. contact, electrical, magnetic, gravitational, weight, tension, friction and air resistance)  the agent applying the force  the object the force is applied to
- `OBJ-6118-2.4-03-M01` tier 1 — Identify contact and normal forces
- `OBJ-6118-2.4-03-M02` tier 1 — Identify an electrical force
- `OBJ-6118-2.4-03-M03` tier 1 — Identify a magnetic force
- `OBJ-6118-2.4-03-M04` tier 1 — Identify gravitational force and weight
- `OBJ-6118-2.4-03-M05` tier 1 — Identify tension
- `OBJ-6118-2.4-03-M06` tier 1 — Identify friction and air resistance
- `OBJ-6118-2.4-03-M07` tier 1 — Identify the force agent and the object acted on
- `OBJ-6118-2.4-04` tier 2 — find the resultant of two or more forces acting along the same line
- `OBJ-6118-2.4-05` tier 2 — plot and interpret extension-load graphs, describe the associated experimental procedure and use proportionality in simple calculations
- `OBJ-6118-2.4-06` tier 2 — state Hooke's law and recall and use the expression F = kx where F is the force applied, k is the spring constant and x is the extension of the spring
- `OBJ-6118-2.4-07` tier 1 — identify the 'limit of proportionality' for an extension-load graph
- `OBJ-6118-2.4-08` tier 2 — Relate resultant force, mass and acceleration using F = ma, including their directions.
- `OBJ-6118-2.4-09` tier 2 — recall and use the formula momentum = mass × velocity
- `OBJ-6118-2.4-10` tier 2 — recall and use the change in momentum = force × time of action
- `OBJ-6118-2.4-11` tier 1 — define the principle of conservation of momentum as when two or more objects interact, their total momentum remains constant, provided that no external resultant force is acting on them
- `OBJ-6118-2.4-12` tier 2 — apply the conservation of momentum in simple applications, including elastic and inelastic collisions between two bodies in one dimension (definitions of an elastic and inelastic collision are not required)
