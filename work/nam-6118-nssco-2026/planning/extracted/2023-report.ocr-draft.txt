GENERAL COMMENTS

Learners need to be taught definitions as they are clearly stated in the syllabus and not definitions from other sources.

Much emphasis should also be paid to mathematical requirements. Learners need to be taught on how to use calculators
as most of them failed to use the second function of sine ratio to get the angle of refraction. Teachers should also allow
learners to practice on drawing ray diagrams using appropriate instruments, the use of protractors and ruler, not free
hand drawings.

Most learners were able to differentiate between how conduction of metals and non-metals takes place.
That was awarded one mark, but they struggled to explain as to what causes the heat conduction
in metallic rod and non - conduction in non — metallic rod which required candidates to refer to free
electrons in metals and absence of free electrons in non - metals. Some candidates referred to free
ions instead of free electrons. Most candidates could not score a mark which demanded them to
mention the collision of electrons with atoms which result in a transfer of heat in a metallic rod.

Majority of the candidates were able to identify the features, however they could not score full marks
because they struggled to identify the methods of heat transfer prevented by each feature. Due to the
fact that candidates failed to identify the method of heat transfer prevented, they also failed to explain
as to how the heat energy is then prevented from being transferred.

Spelling of silver and also referring to other things rather than mentioning silver colour. They referred
to white and also silver pot/silver metal while the main aspect was to identify silver colour in the
thermos flask.

Most learners could not relate the altitude to atmospheric pressure. Instead of comparing altitude with
atmospheric pressure, they referred to pressure only. They also just emphasised on how impurity and
density affect the boiling point of water and not atmospheric pressure which was asked.

Candidates could also not mention that more energy was required at lower altitude and that is why the
boiling point is different.

(i) Poorly answered. Most candidates just defined latent heat instead of giving a molecular
interpretation as it was required by the question. They could not indicate what happens to the
forces between the molecules when energy is either absorbed or released and also refer to the
change in potential energy.

(ii) Well answered. Most candidates could recall and use the correct formula to calculate the energy
using the provided information, although there were few candidates who used the formula for
specific heat capacity instead and also those who did not convert the mass of 2000 g to kg.

(iii) Poorly answered. Many candidates could not be awarded marks because most of them referred

to water having higher boiling point instead of referring to the small volume of water needed.
