spaces at the top of this page.
• Write your answers on the Question Paper in the spaces provided.
• Write in dark blue or black pen.
• You may use a soft pencil for any diagrams, graphs or rough working.
• Do not use correction fluid.
• Do not write in the margin For Examiner’s Use.
• Answer all questions.
• The number of marks is given in brackets [ ] at the end of each question
or part question.
• You will lose marks if you do not show your working or if you do not use
appropriate units.
