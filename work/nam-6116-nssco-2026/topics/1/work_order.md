## Performance tasks

- **multiple_choice_set** — A set of 12 four-option multiple-choice questions on this topic in the style of Paper 1, one mark each: about five in eight on knowledge with understanding and three in eight on handling information or application, each distractor built from a known confusion (the misconception register first), with the key and the reason each distractor is wrong.
- **short_answer** — A Paper 2 style structured question of about 8 marks: four or five parts on one situation, moving from naming or stating (1 mark) through describing (2 marks) to explaining or suggesting about unfamiliar material (2-3 marks), each part with its mark allocation.
- **practical_task** — A Paper 3 (Alternative to Practical) style question of about 10 marks set on an experiment from this topic, described in words with our own readings: identify the variables, complete a table with units in the headings, plot or describe a graph, draw a conclusion from the data, and name one source of error with an improvement tied to it.
