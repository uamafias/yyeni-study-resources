# Geography 6137 — definition decisions for the plan

Original Step 2 flags remain unchanged in source-definition-review-flags-6137.json. This is the planner’s resolution, not an edit to the official PDF.

| Flag | Adopted treatment | Primary support |
|---|---|---|
| Catchment/boundary | Catchment/drainage basin is the contributing area; watershed means the divide in this NSSCO usage. Flag the different US usage. | USGS-BASIN |
| Dependency ratio | Specified dependent-age population ÷ specified working-age population × 100. Common teaching ages under 15 plus 65+, versus 15–64; follow supplied bands. It is not actual employment dependency. | WB-ADR; UN-ADR |
| Infant mortality | Before the first birthday, per 1,000 live births; distinguish estimated probability from a simple observed annual calculation. | WHO-IMR |
| Fertility | Total fertility concerns expected births under the period’s age-specific rates, not biological capacity; distinguish crude birth rate. | WHO-TFR |
| Wood/renewability | Biomass can replenish; unsustainable removal can deplete a local wood resource. Do not call all wood intrinsically non-renewable or all biomass automatically sustainable. | EIA-BIOMASS; SEAI-BIOMASS |

HIV/AIDS/ART senses also use WHO-HIV. NOAA-OZONE supports keeping greenhouse warming separate from ozone depletion. URLs and access date are in primary-definition-sources-6137.json. These are planning definitions and source decisions; the final learner text and any new worked examples still need cross-review.

Other source caveats remain scoped: use original maps/numbers rather than the conflicting 2024 bearing, and avoid the mixed rainforest/fleshy-stem source example. For energy/load examples, specify the system capacity; a source’s scenario-specific limitation is not a universal ban.
