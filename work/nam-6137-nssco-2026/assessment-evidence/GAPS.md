# Geography 6137 — Step 3 evidence gaps and limits

Available: the 2016 syllabus (first examination 2020), all 15 question papers for 2020–2024, Geography sections from five annual examiner-report volumes, and matching 2021–2024 Paper 2 map sheets. Source hashes and exact report page ranges are in extraction-manifest.json and source-inventory.json.

- **No standalone mark schemes:** examiner reports contain answer lists and sometimes explicit allocation rules, but are not a substitute for complete schemes. Twenty curated answer shapes cover 26 marked allocations. Other records deliberately leave `creditworthy_points` empty rather than invent exact marking rules. The full linked report blocks remain available for further task-specific rubric work. A proposed learner rubric beyond this evidence must be labelled judgement and reviewed.
- **2020 Amersfoort map absent:** its 13 marked mapwork allocations (25 marks) are analysed for demand. Map-dependent answer correctness is unverified. Do not invent answers from the report or substitute a different year’s map. The four recovered sheets support original map-skill practice; exam maps remain internal source evidence.
- **2025 papers/reports absent:** no 2025 frequency or misconception claims. The corpus has only five annual series, not five repeated observations of each topic.
- **2026 Paper 3 circular:** the project follows Vitalis’s confirmation of the later circular: Coastal/Marine Processes and Leisure and Tourism. The public mirror and confirmation are recorded separately. No ministry-hosted original/local circular PDF has been obtained. Do not infer these topics from the 2020–2024 table.
- **Extraction fallback:** Poppler is unavailable. pypdf plain and layout extracts were used, with pdfplumber independently reconciling all bracketed marks. PDFium rendered selected ambiguous layouts/figures for inspection. Text extraction does not validate the economics/geography of a diagram or independently solve every map question.
- **Exposure resolution:** the catalogue contains 140 specific-objective bullets and two cross-cutting syllabus aims. Many bullets contain several examples; a hit on one example is not proof that all examples have appeared. Practical methods are mapped to integrated Theme 4 outcomes, with physical/human contexts retained separately. The planner must carry IDs forward or create an audited crosswalk to any finer registry.
- **AO evidence:** official qualification/paper weights are in syllabus-facts.json. Report question-heading labels are recorded where given; other primary-focus labels are miner judgement, not official per-part mark splits. Do not recompute or replace the syllabus grid using them.
- **Report classification:** all source blocks are retained, and substantive weaknesses are classified at block level. A block may contain several weaknesses plus expected answers and teaching advice. These category counts are not counts of individual candidates or independent misconception occurrences. The 27-item misconception register is separately curated; a true but irrelevant answer is not automatically a false belief.
- **Source conflicts:** source-conflicts-6137.json records eight caveats, including disputed map bearings, numbering, context-dependent solar constraints, and scientific/marking ambiguities. The five glossary/content-definition flags from Step 2 also remain in planning/source-definition-review-flags-6137.json. Resolve affected claims against primary sources before authoring the final wording; none is silently copied into learner resources.
- **Review boundary:** this is the miner’s own source/numeric verification. No independent cross-review or learner PUBLISH gate has run, and no notes, cards, plan, upload, commit or push is being claimed.
