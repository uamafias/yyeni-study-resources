China's one-child policy ran from about 1979-80 to 2015: most couples were limited to one child, couples who had extra children could be fined, and rural couples whose first child was a girl could often have a second. Its main demographic concerns were a male-biased sex ratio and rapid ageing. All couples could have two children from 1 January 2016, and on 31 May 2021 the leadership announced that couples could have a third child, with support measures set out in July 2021.

CL-6137-4.2.04-R1
Contour patterns identify landforms: valley contours form Vs pointing towards higher values (upstream), spur contours form Vs pointing towards lower values; a plateau has widely spaced or no contours on top and close contours at its edges; an escarpment is a band of closely spaced contours forming a long steep slope; a ridge is long narrow high ground; a saddle is a dip between two hills; a gorge has very close contours on both sides of a deep valley; an inselberg is an isolated steep-sided hill on a plain. A point on a contour line has that line's height.
Standard map-skills content for syllabus objective 4.2.04 (identify landforms using contours and symbols)

Map descriptions of human activity use map evidence: relief from contours and spot heights, drainage from rivers and their flow from high to low ground, land use from symbols such as cultivated lands, excavations and mine dumps, and settlement sites at junctions, crossings or water sources, each supported by a location or place name.
Standard map-skills content for syllabus objective 4.2.05 (human activities in relation to map features)
