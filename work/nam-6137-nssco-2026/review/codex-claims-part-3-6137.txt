CL-6137-2.5.02-R1
Namibia's five perennial rivers (Kunene, Okavango, Kwando/Linyanti/Chobe, Zambezi and Orange) all lie on or cross its borders and are fed by rain in neighbouring countries; interior rivers are ephemeral and flow only briefly after heavy rain. Major aquifers include the Karst area, Stampriet and Ohangwena, reached by tens of thousands of boreholes and wells. Evaporation (about 2,000 mm a year at the coast to over 3,400 mm in the south-east) far exceeds rainfall.
Namibia national presentation 'National experience on water statistics', UNSD technical workshop on environment statistics, Windhoek, 3-5 December 2019: https://unstats.un.org/unsd/envstats/meetings/2019-Namibia/documents/Session%205.1.1%20Water%20Statistics%20Namibia.pdf (slides 5, 7, 8, 9); Atlas of Namibia, Chapter 4 Water, Key points: https://atlasofnamibia.online/node/144. accessed 2026-10-30
