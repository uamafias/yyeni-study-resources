## Material corrections

1. **Household cooking fuel:** the microdata browser's unweighted case shares were not population household percentages. Replaced 58% wood/29% electricity with NSA's released 2023 census results: 50.1% wood nationally and 84.6% in rural areas. The variable browser itself warns against treating its cases as population statistics.
2. **HIV reasoning:** regional prevalence is not proof that borders, density or an occupation cause infection, nor evidence ranking the main cause. Notes and the judgement package now explain possible exposure/service-access mechanisms and the limits of prevalence comparisons. Ordinary contact does not transmit HIV. The ART card now requires a maintained undetectable viral load for the sexual non-transmission statement. Condom-related birth and survival effects pull growth in different directions; no fixed net growth effect is claimed.
3. **Altitude:** removed 'thin air holds less heat' and the distance-from-land explanation. Rising air expands and cools as pressure falls; the environmental average is distinguished from a parcel cooling rate.
4. **Livestock:** removed the blanket northern overseas-export ban. Movement, quarantine and processing requirements vary with product and destination and can restrict market access. Changed the unsourced assertion that most northern homesteads keep cattle to some households.
5. **Claims versus teaching text:** reconciled the coastal-park overstatement, studied Kuiseb events, northern cattle restrictions and historic Windhoek settlement statistics. The 60%/25% finding concerns the north/north-west area, including formal and informal settlements, in the 2014 study; it is not a 2026 census figure for informal settlements alone.
6. **Water:** perennial rivers can lie along or cross boundaries; interior surface reservoirs also exist. Groundwater reliability depends on usable storage, recharge, quality and extraction. The August 2025 Windhoek judgement is explicitly dated. Desalination inauguration in 2010 is distinguished from NamWater's 2013 supply contract. Bilharzia transmission is explained through parasite larvae and skin contact with infested water. Removed an exact evaporation range that could not be fully confirmed, retaining the supported potential-evaporation/recharge explanation.
7. **Energy and transport:** local generation can displace imports; it does not guarantee every unit reduces overseas payments. Fuel-price effects have exchange-rate/tax/regulation conditions. The Atlas's 45% figure refers to electricity **produced in Namibia**, not total consumption including imports. Air Namibia's closure ended its own Frankfurt service, not every carrier's direct service.
8. **Physical and fieldwork precision:** a pebbleometer measures size; the roundness chart classifies shape. Dune migration and resultant transport are qualified. Longshore direction follows actual wave approach and coastal orientation, not local prevailing wind alone. North-western game-count declines are distinguished from national elephant totals.
9. **Card evidence:** shared use of a waterhole suggests possible competition but does not prove water shortage or grazing competition. Removed answer-revealing settlement-pattern/land-use alt text from question fronts.
