Upwelling is the rise of deeper water towards the surface. Off Namibia the cold Benguela Current flows northwards, driven by the south-east trade winds. A common investigation tests whether footpath erosion decreases with distance from a tourist hot spot such as a car park, viewpoint or lodge. Most visitors walk the first stretch of path, so trampling is concentrated there. Use systematic sampling: place measurement stations at regular intervals, for example every 20 m, along the path away from the hot spot. Regular spacing covers the whole path evenly and avoids choosing only the worst-eroded places; random sampling might leave gaps, while stratified sampling suits paths with distinct sections, such as steep and flat stretches.
