## Specification

Schematic vertical cross-section, not to scale (stated in the title). Ocean on the left, continent on the right. One function gives the upper surface of oceanic plate P: flat abyssal floor, then a quadratic bend whose slope rises steadily to a straight 36.9° dip. The trench is where the front of continental plate Q meets that surface, which makes it the deepest point of the sea floor. The base of plate Q follows the slab top until the slab drops below Q's base, which leaves a mantle wedge. Two volcanoes stand on Q inland from the coast. ## Independent solution before drawing

1. P's arrow points east and Q's points west, towards P, so the plates converge (1).
2. P bends down at the trench and continues below Q into the mantle, so the slab descends from the trench beneath Q (1).
3. Both volcanoes stand on Q, east of the coast and the trench, so they are on the overriding side (1).

All three can be read from the question image without the answer layer.
