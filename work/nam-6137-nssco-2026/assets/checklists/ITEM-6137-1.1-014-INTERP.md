## Independent solution

1. The arrows run parallel to the fault and point in opposite directions (1).
2. The plates therefore slide horizontally past one another along the margin (1).
3. The stream's eastern reach sits north of its western reach along the fault, matching K's northward movement relative to M, so it is consistent with lateral displacement (1).
