## Independent solution

1. The collector and funnel feed a measure in mm, so A measures rainfall depth (1).
2. A dry bulb with a wet bulb gives a depression, from which relative humidity comes via a table (1).
3. The cups rotate the spindle, so C measures wind speed in m/s (1).
