## Independent solution

1. Rainforest lies in separate equatorial blocks (Amazon, Congo, South-east Asia) (1).
2. Savannah is tropical, on several continents, often flanking the forest (1).
3. Deserts lie near the tropics and include west-coast examples (Namib, Atacama). They do not form one band (1).
