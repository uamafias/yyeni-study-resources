The P04 sides were redrawn so the package practises the syllabus method, OBJ-6137-2.3.91 (PDF p. 18): designing a bi-polar grid and choosing its categories, using it to judge a tourism impact, presenting the data as a graph, and considering the time of day and the number of locations.

**What did not change.**
- The base sheet (036) and the P07 sides were left as they were. Their PNGs are byte-identical to the accepted versions; the SHA-256 was checked after the rebuild.
- The 036 card still teaches the simpler 0–2 sheet, which the notes also describe (BL-6137-2.3.91-04).
- The master and spec changed, so the whole resource needs Codex re-acceptance.

**The new P04 design.**
- **Grid:** five paired rows: no litter / litter everywhere; quiet / very noisy; plants undamaged / plants trampled; uncrowded / very crowded; facilities clean / facilities dirty (Codex changed the negative pole from "broken" on 30 October 2026, because clean/broken was not a true opposite pair; Claude confirmed).

  - The positive description is always on the left.
  - A site total runs from −10 to +10.
- **Sites:** S1 to S5, placed every 100 m (0–400 m) along a path from a car-park hot spot, all surveyed 10:00–10:30 on one weekday.
- **Results:**
