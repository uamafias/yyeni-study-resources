## Specification

**Projection.** Equirectangular, with longitude scaled by cos 22.5°. The scale bar is true north–south; east–west distances are within about 5 %, and the figure states this.

**Question map.**
- **Farming classes (four):** dryland crops with livestock; mainly cattle; cattle with sheep and goats (striped); mainly sheep and goats (dotted). Areas with little or no farming are left white.
- **Crop fields:** dark cells where fields cover at least 15 % of the land.
- **Commercial farms:** the edge of the commercial farmland, drawn as a dark line.
- **Crop area:** the hatched Otavi–Grootfontein–Tsumeb triangle.
- **Irrigation:** blue points, keyed "Irrigation area (location)". No claim is made about current operation.
- **Places:** towns and region boundaries. The four north-central regions are labelled in bold with leader lines; nine other regions are labelled in grey italics.
- **Furniture:** north arrow, 0–300 km scale bar, key, and a source line with the licences.
- **Card 015 inset:** titled "plots beside a northern town (schematic, not a surveyed farm)". It shows one plot keyed "Vegetables for sale" beside the town and road, and four plots keyed "Cultivated: purpose not shown".

**Answer sides.** Each answer side adds markers and an interpretation panel; the map geometry is unchanged.

## Visual inspection

Every side was checked at full size, at 390 px and in greyscale. Fixes made after inspection:
- **Data choices:**
  - The north-central 1997 cleared-land grid was too coarse (a rectangular block), so I gridded the national layer instead.
  - The class labels were renamed "Mainly cattle" and so on, because the source classes some communal northern land as cattle.
- **Greyscale contrast:** the crop yellow and the cattle green were darkened, because yellow and white were too close in greyscale.
- **Label clashes:**
  - The Kavango West/East labels were dropped: they clashed with Tsumeb, and no card needs them.
  - The labels for Tsumeb, Keetmanshoop, //Karas, Zambezi and Omaheke were moved.
  - Answer markers were moved off the Rundu and Keetmanshoop town symbols and the Kunene label.
- **Answer wording:**
  - The 014 answer first said "much of Ohangwena". The data shows the west only, so it was corrected.
  - Rivers are not drawn on the map, so the answers say "northern border near Rundu" and "southern border (the Orange River)".
- **Inset:** marker 2 was added to the 015 inset.

## Limits for the reviewer

- **Data dates:** the farming-system areas are from 2006 (Mendelsohn). The national cleared-land file records no survey date; the Atlas's regional grids date from 1996 to 2018. The irrigation points come from the same 2006/NAU data. The figure does not claim current operation or a current crop season.
- **Data licence:** the Atlas states CC BY 4.0 for the book. The data downloads are the Atlas's own published data.
- **Scale:** the farm-cadastre union drops fragments smaller than 0.05 square degrees, and the farming classes are clipped to the NSA outline.
