## Independent solution

1. CO₂ in the air and soil dissolves in the rainwater, forming a weak carbonic acid (1).
2. The water follows the joint into the limestone and contacts and reacts with the calcium carbonate (1).
3. Dissolved material leaves in the water, and the joint has grown from 3 mm to 30 mm over many years, so dissolution enlarges it (1).
