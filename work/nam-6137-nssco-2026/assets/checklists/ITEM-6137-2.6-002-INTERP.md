## Visual inspection

Every side was checked at full size, at 390 px and in greyscale; the parks, the hatched conservancies and the star stay distinct in greyscale. Fixes made after inspection:
- **Key:** the two key columns overlapped, so the key became one column.
- **Otjiwarongo label:** it ran into the Torra outline (misleading proximity), so it moved east of its symbol.
- **Other labels and markers:** the Zambezi and Omaheke labels and the Torra and CCF markers were moved.
- **Key wording:** the key's "a point, not an area" gave away a mark and was removed.
- **Answer wording:** it now says the 40 km is a straight-line distance. The notes keep "about 44 km" and the new sentence says the road is a little farther.

## Limits for the reviewer

- The CCF position is the GeoNames farm point, not a surveyed building location.
- The conservancy data date from 2020: 89 records. The existing notes cite 86 conservancies in 2022 from NACSO. The key says "registered communal conservancy (2020)".
- In Zambezi, the Atlas uses gazetted rather than working boundaries (Mashi, Mudumu, Nkasa Rupara). Neither named feature is affected.
