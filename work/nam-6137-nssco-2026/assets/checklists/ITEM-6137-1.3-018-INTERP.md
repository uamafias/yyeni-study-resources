## Independent solution

1. The abrupt 4 m drop is a waterfall, and the hollow at its foot is a plunge pool (1).
2. The overhang shows the weaker rock cut back under the cap, and the cap-rock blocks in the pool show that the unsupported cap collapses (1).
3. Repeated collapse moves the lip upstream, leaving the narrow, steep-sided reach (b, c) downstream as the gorge (1).
