## Specification

Code: `assets/code/r_4_2_04.py`. The projection is in `map42.tm`.

**Projection.**
- Transverse Mercator on WGS84 (Snyder 1987, eqs 8-9 to 8-13), central meridian 17°01′E, origin 23°01′S, k0 = 1.
- The scale is 1:50,000 when printed 20 cm wide.

**Graticule.**
- Lines every 30″, from 23°00′00″S to 23°02′00″S and from 17°00′00″E to 17°02′00″E.
- Each line is projected point by point, not drawn as a linear grid relabelled in degrees.
- Full-minute labels sit on the left and bottom; half-minute labels on the right and top.

**Features and labels.**
- P and Q are plotted from their coordinates, and the coordinates are also listed.
- Orientation, key and scale: true north (along the meridians, since convergence is about 0.007°), a key, and a 0–1 km bar.
- The note states the projection, the physical size, and that this is an exercise extent, not a named place.

**Answer.**
- Q is 1′ south and 1′ east of P.
- The midpoint is M, 23°01′00″S, 17°01′00″E.
- 1′ of latitude ≈ 1.85 km, but 1′ of longitude ≈ 1.71 km here.
- 23°01′30″ = 23.025° S (−23.025°), not 23.0130.
