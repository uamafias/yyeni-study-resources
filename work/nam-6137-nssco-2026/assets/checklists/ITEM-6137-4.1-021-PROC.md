## Specification

Code: `assets/code/r_4_1_01.py`. It matches notes BL-6137-4.1.04-02, -05 and -09.
- **Panel A:** twenty numbered eligible sites. Five were drawn by chance without replacement: 3, 8, 11, 16 and 19 (filled).
- **Panel B:** a 100 m line, sampled from a stated start of 0 m at a fixed interval of 10 m (11 points).
- **Panel C:** two defined zones, with 8 and 12 numbered sites. Three sites were drawn by chance within each zone: zone 1 → 2, 5, 7; zone 2 → 3, 9, 12.
- **Key:** chosen site, eligible site not chosen, point on the line.
- **Question side:** names no method (random, systematic and stratified are forbidden words there).
- **Answer side:** labels A random, B systematic, C stratified, with the procedure for each and a reminder to define the frame first and record the sites chosen, any replacements and the limits.
- **P04 sheet:**
  - a plan of stations A 0 m, B 50 m and C 100 m on a dashed safe route along the upper beach, with "Sea: no entry into the water";
  - a table of pebble longest dimensions (10/12/14, 20/22/24, 30/32/34 mm) and falls of 40, 50 and 25 cm over a 10 m run, with the mean column blank;
  - unit notes.

  The answer fills in 12, 22 and 32 mm: A and C are part (j), and B is the part (d) calculation.
- **P05 form:**
  - a respondent form with a group screening question, a voluntary-consent box, a neutral question read as written, no names, and refusals recorded separately;
  - a tally with residents yes 12 / no 8 and visitors yes 16 / no 4 / respondents 20; the resident respondents and both percentages are blank.

  The answer gives residents 20 respondents and 60%, and visitors 80%.

## Independent solution

- **021:** define the eligible frame, then select by chance (A) (1); state a start and an interval (B) (1); define the zones, then apply a within-zone rule, and record the sites chosen and the limits (C) (1). This agrees with the card's answer.
- **P04 (j):** A (10+12+14)/3 = 12 mm and C (30+32+34)/3 = 32 mm; **(d):** B = 22 mm. The gradients are 1000/40 = 1:25, 1:20 and 1:40, matching the card's (e).
- **P05 (j):** 12/20 = 60%, with a denominator of twenty residents; **(d):** 16/20 = 80%.
