## Independent solution

1. R's arrow points west and S's east, away from the centre line, so the plates move apart (1).
2. Magma fills the fissure up to the opening, so magma reaches the opening, where it cools and solidifies (1).
3. The darkest (newest) crust lies at the centre and lighter (older) bands lie symmetrically farther out, so new crust forms at the opening and is carried outward (1).
