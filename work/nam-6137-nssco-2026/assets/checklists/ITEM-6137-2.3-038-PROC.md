## Revision, 30 October 2026: P05 rebuilt for erosion against distance from a hot spot (handover §3)

The P05 sides were redrawn so the package practises the syllabus method, OBJ-6137-2.3.92 (PDF p. 19): measuring footpath erosion against distance from a tourism hot spot, with decisions on sampling and measurement.

**What did not change.**
- The base plan, section and record sheet (038) are unchanged. Their PNGs are byte-identical; the SHA-256 was checked.
- The master and spec changed, so the whole resource needs Codex re-acceptance.

**The new P05 design.**
- **Layout:** the viewpoint car park (hot spot) with stations S1–S5 every 30 m (0–120 m, systematic sampling). The S4 field note reads "wet, muddy hollow".
- **Method, stated on the image:**
  - width is three tape readings at right angles, edge to edge, with the edge defined;
  - depth is the mean of five equally spaced ruler readings below a pole laid across the path.
- **Record sheet:**

| Station | Distance | Widths (cm) | Mean width | Mean depth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | 0 m | 215, 220, 225 | 220 | 8.0 |
| S2 | 30 m | 165, 170, 175 | **170** (blank) | 6.4 |
| S3 | 60 m | 130, 140, 150 | 140 | **4.2** (blank; readings 3.8, 4.4, 4.0, 4.6, 4.2) |
| S4 | 90 m | 150, 155, 160 | **155** (blank) | 3.0 |
| S5 | 120 m | 90, 95, 100 | 95 | 1.6 |

- **Axes:** distance 0–120 m against mean width 0–250 cm. S1, S3 and S5 are plotted on the question side; the answer side adds S2 (30, 170) and S4 (90, 155).
- **No visitor counts.** The card states that the survey compares present path form with distance and does not measure visitor use or change over time. Codex corrected my earlier proxy wording on 30 October 2026; Claude confirmed.

**Independent solution.**
- H1 (width decreases at every station) is not fully supported: S3 140 → S4 155 at the wet hollow.
- H2 (depth decreases with distance) is supported: 8.0, 6.4, 4.2, 3.0, 1.6.
- The P05 card was rewritten to match. All 12 parts keep their tariffs and AOs, and it now cites CL-6137-2.3.92-R1 and R2.

**Checks.**
- Automated: the means, the S3 depth, the width exception, the falling depth, the equal intervals, and fit within the panel.
- The question side is forbidden from showing "(30, 170)", "(90, 155)", "H1" or "supported".
