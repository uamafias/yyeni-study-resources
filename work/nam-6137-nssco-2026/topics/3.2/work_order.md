## Performance tasks

- **short_answer** — Seven-mark original thematic/resource mini-package: short knowledge and explanation parts totalling 5 A marks plus two evidence-reading B marks. Choose live commands and exact tariffs for each part. This is a teaching package, not a copied whole question. In integrated enquiry use content-linked interpretation, not a supposed Paper 1 fieldwork-method question.
- **data_response** — Seven-mark original map/photograph/graph/data package, tailored to this topic: one knowledge mark, five analytical/resource marks and one supported decision mark. Supply all resources and keys needed. In map skills use a consistent 1:50000 map; use actual licensed photographs where the outcome names photographs.
- **case_analysis** — Five-mark judgement on a precise geographical claim with a verified named case where appropriate. Use How far do you agree; judge relevant facts and reach a supported decision. Four marks judge relevant facts and one credits the supported decision, which may be implied. Apply the 2024 demand without forcing equal-sided essays. In integrated enquiry apply the judgement to the supplied investigation evidence.
