# NSSCO Mathematics — Workbench resources

This is the learning-resources authoring home for calculation-based Workout Bench
questions. The app consumes an explicitly exported, checksum-pinned copy.

## Published package

- **409 recipe settings / 410 assignment choices / 4,022 saved examples.**
- `workbench/package/` contains 2,116 pinned generator, reference, marking,
  diagram, lesson-routing and seed-plan files, including 1,992 prepared PNGs.
- The 152 syllabus-bullet locators and 440 authored demand entries have explicit
  assessed-part evidence. Mapping presence does not certify full coverage.
- `workbench/tools/` verifies and exports the package. `workbench/reviews/` and
  `workbench/repairs/` preserve review evidence and historical repair baselines.
- Authoring begins with Yeni's existing learner-facing guidelines; see the
  [authoring workflow](workbench/README.md).

R9/R10 focused reviews accepted the original repairs and the explained retained
choices. The final parallel-locus label adjustment has local verification after
R10. Full-bank mathematical/tariff approval and whole-syllabus sufficiency remain
pending. New cosmetic suggestions are recorded separately. This publication
makes resources available in the repo; it does not deploy the app or import
learner cards automatically.

## Preservation and scope

The old 3,300-card bank and separate 420-card Statistics bank/import dependencies
were removed only after verifying Jeremy's Git authorship/committer records.
Original review packets and learner snapshots remain intact.

Older local analysis, flashcard drafts, prototype curriculum files and scratch
links with unverified authorship are preserved locally, outside this publication.
They are not active Workbench inputs. The repo's existing official source
collections and the packaged objective locators remain the source references.
The subject audit/provenance records are under `operations/review/namibia-nssco-6131-*`.
