## What this means for mining

Mining can proceed on 84 paper-and-mark-scheme pairs, which is ample for mapping command words, mark tariffs, AO focus and question contexts to objectives. The gap is narrower and more specific than before: **examiner-evidenced misconceptions for 2024–2025 are unavailable**, so the assessment layer (pipeline Stage 12) can link to examiner findings for 2020–2023 only. Record that limitation in the depth plan rather than letting it pass silently.

## Syllabus alignment of the corpus

Syllabus v2 states there are no significant content changes from the 2023 edition, so series from 2023 onward are aligned with the 2026–2028 scope. Series from 2020–2022 predate the 2023 revision and are flagged `series_predates_2023_syllabus_revision`. Both are usable, but weight the newer series when calibrating emphasis, and check any 2020–2022 question against the current syllabus before treating it as in scope.

## Blocking dependency

Mining cannot start until the **Stage 3 objective registry** exists: every record in `assessment_evidence.json` requires at least one objective ID (`objective_ids`, `minItems: 1`), and there are no objective IDs yet. Building the registry for topics 1.1–5.5 is the next step.
