## Performance tasks

- **practical_task** — A Paper 3 Question 1 style task (20 marks): an experiment described in words, with a set of our readings of the independent and dependent variables. The learner says how each reading would be taken and repeated, draws up the table (quantity / unit headings, a calculated column, consistent raw data, justified significant figures), plots the graph on their own graph paper against the stated conditions, finds the gradient and the intercept, and uses them to find the constants of a given equation, with units.
- **practical_task** — A Paper 3 Question 2 style task (20 marks): an experiment described in words whose method is deliberately inaccurate, with two sets of our readings. The learner estimates an absolute uncertainty from repeats and turns it into a percentage uncertainty, calculates a constant twice and tests a suggested relationship against a criterion they state first, then gives four limitations and four improvements, each tied to the measurement it affects.
- **practical_task** — A second Paper 3 Question 1 style task (20 marks), set in a different area of physics from the first (if the first is mechanical, this one is electrical, optical or thermal): the same table, graph, gradient, intercept and constants, from a different experiment described in words with our readings.
- **practical_task** — A second Paper 3 Question 2 style task (20 marks), in a different area of physics from the first: an inaccurate method with two sets of our readings, a percentage uncertainty, a test of a suggested relationship against a stated criterion, and four limitations with four matching improvements.
