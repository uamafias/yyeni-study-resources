## Content units

**CU-9702-7.1** — sub-topic 7.1, 7 objectives, 2117–3031 words
- `OBJ-9702-7.1-01` tier 2 — describe what is meant by wave motion as illustrated by vibration in ropes, springs and ripple tanks

- `OBJ-9702-7.1-03` tier 2 — understand the use of the time-base and y-gain of a cathode-ray oscilloscope (CRO) to determine frequency and amplitude

- `OBJ-9702-7.1-05` tier 2 — recall and use v = fλ
- `OBJ-9702-7.1-06` tier 2 — understand that energy is transferred by a progressive wave
- `OBJ-9702-7.1-07` tier 2 — recall and use intensity = power/area and intensity ∝ (amplitude)2 for a progressive wave

- `OBJ-9702-7.2-02` tier 3 — analyse and interpret graphical representations of transverse and longitudinal waves

**CU-9702-7.3** — sub-topic 7.3, 2 objectives, 605–866 words

- `OBJ-9702-7.4-03` tier 2 — recall that wavelengths in the range 400–700 nm in free space are visible to the human eye

- `OBJ-9702-7.5-01` tier 2 — understand that polarisation is a phenomenon associated with transverse waves
- `OBJ-9702-7.5-02` tier 2 — recall and use Malus’s law (I = I0 cos²θ) to calculate the intensity of a plane-polarised electromagnetic wave after transmission through a polarising filter or a series of polarising filters

## Performance tasks

- **multiple_choice_set** — A set of 12 four-option multiple-choice questions on this topic in the style of Paper 1, one mark each: about half recall and half application or calculation, every distractor built from a known error (the misconception register first), the key and the reason each distractor is wrong on the back.
- **short_answer** — A Paper 2 style structured question: four or five linked parts around one situation (define or state, calculate, explain), about 8 marks, each part with its own mark allocation.
- **calculation** — A multi-step calculation set in a practical or everyday situation (about 5 marks): the equation in symbols, the substitution, each intermediate value to three significant figures, the answer with its unit and a justified number of significant figures.
