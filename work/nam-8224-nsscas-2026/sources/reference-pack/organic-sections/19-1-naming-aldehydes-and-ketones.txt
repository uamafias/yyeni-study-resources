19.1 • Naming Aldehydes and Ketones
Aldehydes are named by replacing the terminal -
e
of the corresponding alkane name with -
al.
The parent chain must contain the –CHO group, and the –CHO carbon is numbered as carbon 1. Note in the following examples that the longest chain in 2-ethyl-4-methylpentanal is actually a hexane, but this chain does not include the –CHO group and thus is not the parent.
For cyclic aldehydes in which the –CHO group is directly attached to a ring, the ring name followed by
carbaldehyde
is used.
