Although more stable than a typical alkyl carbocation because of resonance, the intermediate in electrophilic aromatic substitution is nevertheless much less stable than the starting benzene ring itself, with its 150 kJ/mol (36 kcal/mol) of aromatic stability. Thus, the reaction of an electrophile with a benzene ring is endergonic, has a substantial activation energy, and is rather slow.

Why does the reaction of Br

If addition occurred, the 150 kJ/mol stabilization energy of the aromatic ring would be lost and the overall reaction would be endergonic.
