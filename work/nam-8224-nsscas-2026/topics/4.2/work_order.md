**CU-8224-4.2.2** — sub-topic 4.2.2, 13 objectives, 3466–4977 words
- `OBJ-8224-4.2.2-01` tier 2 — interpret and use the general, structural, displayed and skeletal formulae of the alkenes
- `OBJ-8224-4.2.2-02` tier 1 — recall and use systematic nomenclature to name, alkenes, with chain length up to six carbon atoms, , e.g. but-1-ene, but-2-ene
- `OBJ-8224-4.2.2-03` tier 3 — deduce the molecular formula of alkenes, given its structural, displayed or skeletal formula
- `OBJ-8224-4.2.2-04` tier 2 — describe the chemistry of alkenes as exemplified, where relevant, by the following reactions of ethene and propene (including the Markovnikov addition of asymmetric electrophiles to alkenes using propene as an example): - addition of hydrogen, steam, hydrogen halides and halogens - oxidation by cold, dilute, acidified manganate(VII) ions to form the diol - oxidation by hot, concentrated, acidified manganate(VII) ions leading to the rupture of the carbon–carbon double bond in order to determine the position of alkene linkages in larger molecules - polymerisation
- `OBJ-8224-4.2.2-05` tier 2 — describe the concept heterolytic fission
- `OBJ-8224-4.2.2-06` tier 3 — describe the mechanism of electrophilic addition in alkenes, using bromine/ethene and hydrogen bromide/propene as examples
- `OBJ-8224-4.2.2-07` tier 3 — describe and explain the inductive effects of alkyl groups on the stability of cations formed during electrophilic addition
- `OBJ-8224-4.2.2-08` tier 2 — describe the characteristics of addition polymerisation as exemplified by poly(ethene) and PVC
- `OBJ-8224-4.2.2-09` tier 3 — deduce the repeat unit of an addition polymer obtained from a given monomer
- `OBJ-8224-4.2.2-10` tier 2 — identify the monomer(s) present in a given section of an addition polymer molecule
- `OBJ-8224-4.2.2-11` tier 2 — recognise the difficulty of the disposal of poly(alkene)s, i.e. non-biodegradability
- `OBJ-8224-4.2.2-12` tier 2 — describe ways/ discuss approaches to dealing with waste polymers including manufacturing of biodegradable polymers
- `OBJ-8224-4.2.2-13` tier 2 — recognise that new polymers are being developed, including biodegradable and water soluble polymers

**CU-8224-4.2.3** — sub-topic 4.2.3, 3 objectives, 800–1148 words
