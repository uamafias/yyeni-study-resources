## Content units

**CU-8224-4.3.1** — sub-topic 4.3.1, 4 objectives, 1035–1484 words

- `OBJ-8224-4.3.1-02` tier 1 — recall and use systematic nomenclature to name halogenoalkanes, with chain length up to six carbon atoms, including: naming halogeno groups in alphabetical order, e.g. 1,2-dibromo, 1-chlorobutane

- `OBJ-8224-4.3.2-05` tier 2 — explain the terms bond energy, bond length and bond polarity and use them to compare the reactivities of covalent bonds
