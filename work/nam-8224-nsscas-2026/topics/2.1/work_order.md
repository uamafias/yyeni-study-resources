## Content units

**CU-8224-2.1.1** — sub-topic 2.1.1, 6 objectives, 1786–2560 words
- `OBJ-8224-2.1.1-01` tier 2 — explain that chemical reactions are accompanied by energy changes, principally in the form of heat energy; the energy changes can be exothermic (ΔH is negative) or endothermic (ΔH is positive)
- `OBJ-8224-2.1.1-02` tier 2 — explain and use the terms: - enthalpy change of reaction and standard conditions, with particular reference ret to: formation, combustion, hydration, solution, neutralisation, atomisation - bond energy (ΔH positive, i.e. bond breaking)
- `OBJ-8224-2.1.1-03` tier 2 — calculate enthalpy changes from appropriate experimental results, including the use of the relationship enthalpy change, ΔH = –mcΔT
- `OBJ-8224-2.1.1-04` tier 2 — outline chemical reactions in terms of energy transfers associated with the breaking and making of chemical bonds
- `OBJ-8224-2.1.1-05` tier 2 — calculate energy changes for simple reactions using bond energies
- `OBJ-8224-2.1.1-06` tier 2 — explain why ΔH values from calculated bond energies differ from those obtained from data booklet and experimentally determined values

**CU-8224-2.1.2** — sub-topic 2.1.2, 3 objectives, 893–1280 words
- `OBJ-8224-2.1.2-01` tier 2 — apply Hess‟ law to construct simple energy cycles, and carry out calculations ions involving such cycles and relevant energy terms
- `OBJ-8224-2.1.2-02` tier 2 — apply Hess‟ law to determine enthalpy changes that cannot be found by direct experiment, e.g. an enthalpy change of formation from enthalpy changes of combustion
- `OBJ-8224-2.1.2-03` tier 3 — construct and interpret a reaction pathway diagram, in terms of the enthalpy change of the reaction and of the activation energy
