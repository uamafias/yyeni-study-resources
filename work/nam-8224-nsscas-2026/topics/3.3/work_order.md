## Content units

**CU-8224-3.3.1** — sub-topic 3.3.1, 4 objectives, 1014–1458 words
- `OBJ-8224-3.3.1-01` tier 2 — explain what is meant by a transition element in terms of d-block elements forming one or more ions with incomplete d orbitals
- `OBJ-8224-3.3.1-02` tier 2 — represent the electronic configuration of transition elements using s, p, d and electrons in boxes notations (see section 1.2.3)
- `OBJ-8224-3.3.1-03` tier 2 — recognise that transition elements have variable oxidation states, act as catalysts and form coloured compounds and that these properties are linked to their incomplete d orbitals
- `OBJ-8224-3.3.1-04` tier 2 — contrast qualitatively the melting points and densities of the transition elements with those of calcium as a typical s-block element

**CU-8224-3.3.2** — sub-topic 3.3.2, 7 objectives, 1775–2551 words
- `OBJ-8224-3.3.2-01` tier 2 — recognise that copper, manganese and uranium are produced from ores in Namibia
- `OBJ-8224-3.3.2-02` tier 2 — describe the reduction by carbon of metals from their ores
- `OBJ-8224-3.3.2-03` tier 2 — describe the use of carbon as a reducer, including in the production of (impure) copper from copper oxide and (impure) ferromanganese from a mixture of iron oxide and manganese oxide in Namibia
- `OBJ-8224-3.3.2-04` tier 2 — describe the use of sulfuric acid as a leaching agent for example in the production of copper, manganese and uranium
- `OBJ-8224-3.3.2-05` tier 2 — describe the extraction of pure metals, including electrolysis of leached solutions and the reduction of uranium ions using magnesium
- `OBJ-8224-3.3.2-06` tier 2 — recognize that it is important to monitor and control waste from metal extraction including: - sulfur dioxide as a pollutant product from the roasting of metal ores - waste acids from leaching processes - toxic metal waste in water and as atmospheric particles
- `OBJ-8224-3.3.2-07` tier 2 — discuss the finite nature of metal ores and the benefits and problems of recycling metals
