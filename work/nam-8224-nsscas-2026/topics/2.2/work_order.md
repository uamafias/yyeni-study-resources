**CU-8224-2.2.1** — sub-topic 2.2.1, 5 objectives, 1400–2010 words

**CU-8224-2.2.2** — sub-topic 2.2.2, 5 objectives, 1400–2010 words
- `OBJ-8224-2.2.2-01` tier 2 — describe electrolysis as the conduction of electricity by an ionic compound (an electrolyte), when molten or dissolved in water, leading to the decomposition of the electrolyte
- `OBJ-8224-2.2.2-02` tier 2 — use the concept of selective discharge to identify the electrode products of aqueous solutions to include: - hydrogen ions and metal cations: silver, copper, (hydrogen), iron, zinc, sodium - non-metal anions hydroxide and chloride in dilute and concentrated solutions
- `OBJ-8224-2.2.2-03` tier 2 — describe in outline the use of electrolysis in the extraction of metals from acidic leached solutions, for example copper and manganese
- `OBJ-8224-2.2.2-04` tier 2 — construct ionic half-equations for reactions at the cathode and anode
- `OBJ-8224-2.2.2-05` tier 2 — describe the transfer of charge during electrolysis to include: - the movement of electrons in the metallic conductor - the removal or addition of electrons from the external circuit at the electrodes - the movement of ions in the electrolyte
