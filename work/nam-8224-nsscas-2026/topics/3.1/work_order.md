- `OBJ-8224-3.1.3-03` tier 2 — describe the reactions of the oxides with water

- `OBJ-8224-3.1.3-06` tier 3 — interpret the variations and trends of the above in terms of bonding and electronegativity
- `OBJ-8224-3.1.3-07` tier 3 — suggest the types of chemical bonding present in chlorides and oxides from observations of their chemical and physical properties
