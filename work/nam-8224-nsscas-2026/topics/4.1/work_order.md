## Content units

**CU-8224-4.1.1** — sub-topic 4.1.1, 2 objectives, 572–820 words
- `OBJ-8224-4.1.1-01` tier 3 — interpret and use the following terminology associated with organic reactions: - functional group - homolytic fission and heterolytic fission - free radical, initiation, propagation, termination - nucleophile, electrophile - addition, substitution, elimination, hydrolysis, condensation - oxidation and reduction
- `OBJ-8224-4.1.1-02` tier 1 — recall and use systematic nomenclature to name alkanes, alkenes, halogenoalkanes, alcohols, aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids, esters and amines, with chain length up to six carbon atoms, including: - numbering the carbon atoms in a direction to give the lowest prefix possible e.g. 1-chlorobutane (not 4-chlorobutane) - the use of the prefixes mono-, di-, tri-, tetra-, penta-, hexa-. (in equations for organic redox reactions, the symbols [O] and [H] are acceptable for oxidising and reducing agents)

- `OBJ-8224-4.1.2-02` tier 2 — predict the shapes of, and bond angles in other related molecules

## Performance tasks

- **multiple_choice_set** — A set of 5 original four-option questions for this topic, about half recall and half application or calculation, with a key and an explanation of every distractor.
- **short_answer** — An original structured response with linked 1-3 mark parts using the topic's observed command words and tariffs, about 8 marks.
- **calculation** — An original multi-step calculation or quantitative interpretation, about 5 marks, with conversions, working, units and justified significant figures.
