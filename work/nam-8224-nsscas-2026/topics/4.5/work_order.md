## Content units

**CU-8224-4.5** — sub-topic 4.5, 6 objectives, 1920–2740 words
- `OBJ-8224-4.5-01` tier 3 — deduce (draw and name) the possible isomers for an organic molecule of known molecular formula
- `OBJ-8224-4.5-02` tier 2 — describe structural isomerism and its division into chain, positional and functional group isomerism
- `OBJ-8224-4.5-03` tier 2 — describe stereoisomerism and its division into geometrical (cis-trans) and optical isomerism (use of E, Z nomenclature is acceptable but is not required)
- `OBJ-8224-4.5-04` tier 2 — describe geometrical (cis-trans) isomerism in alkenes, and explain its origin in terms of restricted rotation due to the presence of double bonds
- `OBJ-8224-4.5-05` tier 2 — explain what is meant by a chiral centre and that such a centre normally gives rise to optical isomerism (NB: Learners should appreciate that compounds can contain more than one chiral centre, but knowledge of meso compounds, or nomenclature such as diastereoisomers is not required)
- `OBJ-8224-4.5-06` tier 2 — identify chiral centres and geometrical (cis-trans) isomerism in a molecule of given structural formula
