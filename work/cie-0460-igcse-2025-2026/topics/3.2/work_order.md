## Performance tasks

- **case_analysis** — A 7-mark case-study answer in the style of the last part of a Paper 1 question: one precise question on the topic's case study, answered with a named place at the right scale, developed and linked points, and place-specific detail. The guidance gives the three levels (simple statements; developed statements with a named example, capped at 5 without one; comprehensive statements with place-specific detail). Where the question asks how far a strategy succeeded, the judgement carries part of the marks.
- **data_response** — A resource-based question in the style of the opening parts of a Paper 1 question (about 8 marks): a table of data, a map extract described as a grid, or a described photograph, with parts of 1, 2, 3 and 2 marks that identify, describe from the resource, and suggest reasons.
