## Performance tasks

- **practical_task** — A Paper 4 style enquiry on physical geography (30 marks), such as a river or a beach study: two hypotheses, the equipment and method with siting and sampling, a table of the students' results, a graph to complete, a conclusion on each hypothesis with paired data, and an evaluation with specific improvements.
- **practical_task** — A Paper 4 style enquiry on human geography (30 marks), such as a pedestrian count, a land-use survey or a questionnaire: two hypotheses, the questionnaire or recording sheet and its sampling, a pilot survey, a table of results, a graph to complete, a conclusion with paired data, and an evaluation with specific improvements.
- **practical_task** — A second physical enquiry (30 marks) in a different setting (weather or a coastline if the first was a river): hypotheses, instruments and their siting, results, a graph to complete, conclusions with paired data, evaluation.
- **practical_task** — A second human enquiry (30 marks) in a different setting (tourism, an environmental quality survey or traffic): hypotheses, the sampling method, a recording sheet, results, a graph to complete, conclusions with paired data, evaluation and an extension.
- **practical_task** — A third physical enquiry (30 marks), set where the first two were not (a river, a beach, weather, soils or vegetation): hypotheses, the method with its equipment and sampling, results, a graph to complete, conclusions with paired data, evaluation.
- **practical_task** — A third human enquiry (30 marks), set where the first two were not (a CBD, a village's services, a sphere of influence, housing quality): hypotheses, a questionnaire and its design, results, a graph to complete, conclusions with paired data, evaluation and an extension.
