# Recall decision rule and October 29 corrections

Status: author judgement awaiting independent review; publication remains on hold. This supersedes the 23.31% headline, not the independent review requirement.

The syllabus, PDF viewer page 41 (printed 38), places numerical manipulation under AO B. PDF page 43 (printed 40) gives approximate A/B/C weights and the 25% recall ceiling. It does not say that every familiar calculation is recall. Our operational interpretation is:

- Reproducing a fact, formula, test, named reagent/product or procedure without executing or interpreting it earns recall credit.
- Selecting and executing operations on supplied quantities earns numerical-application credit, even if the formula is familiar. Single-step substitution is basic application under this rule; its low difficulty must not be confused with high reasoning demand. A separately credited statement of the formula is recall.
- A command such as Explain does not change demand. A standalone familiar fact is recall; a credited cause-and-consequence chain is understanding. Where the response mixes factual and applied credit, count the factual marks separately.
- A supplied-data lookup is information retrieval from the question, not memorised subject knowledge. Its difficulty can still be very low.

This interpretation requires independent agreement. Counting every executed familiar calculation as recall is a different policy and would change the total materially; the current percentage is not independent certification of syllabus compliance.
