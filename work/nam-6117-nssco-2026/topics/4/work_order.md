## Content units

**CU-6117-4.1** — sub-topic 4.1, 8 objectives, 1840–2640 words
- `OBJ-6117-4.1-01` tier 1 — define relative atomic mass, Ar , of an element as the ratio of the average mass of the atoms of the naturally occurring element to 1/12 of the mass of a carbon-12 atom
- `OBJ-6117-4.1-02` tier 1 — define relative formula mass, Mr, of a molecule or chemical compound as the sum of the relative atomic masses of all atoms present in that molecule or compound
- `OBJ-6117-4.1-03` tier 1 — state that ‘a mole’ is an amount of substance containing approximately 6.022 x 1023 particles
- `OBJ-6117-4.1-04` tier 1 — state that the number of particles in one mole of any substance (approx. 6.022 x 1023) is called the Avogadro constant
- `OBJ-6117-4.1-05` tier 1 — state that one mole of any substance has a mass equal to the relative mass in grams of that substance
- `OBJ-6117-4.1-06` tier 1 — state that the empirical formula is the simplest whole number ratio of the elements in a compound
- `OBJ-6117-4.1-07` tier 1 — define empirical formula mass as the relative formula mass, Mr, of a molecule or chemical compound in its simplest form
- `OBJ-6117-4.1-08` tier 1 — define concentration as mass per volume or moles per volume (in gdm3 and moldm3) (Note: The word molarity expresses the concentration of a solution only in moldm3 and is no longer in use)
