## 3. Answer shapes

Card slots carry objective-linked answer-shape templates, scope/variant and an allocation rule. Most templates are not completed credit points: the author must supply the original stimulus, answer and explicit credited elements, especially for APP and INTERP. The 32 Topic 4 calculation variants have fixed checked answers; activity parts have more specific credit briefs, while MC keys and distractors remain to be authored. Machine keyword checks remain a separate structural aid and do not substitute for an answer rubric. Drawing tasks use the supported APP subtype with `representation_task: true`, a Draw/Sketch command and required visuals; DIAGRAM is not a supported learner-item subtype.

The cm³-to-dm³ diagnostic variant cites ER-6117-2022-19.
Each performance task fixes objective IDs, paper, context, subpart commands, tariffs, AO/Bloom labels, answer requirements and visuals. Six-mark investigation rubrics must separately allocate setup, controls, measurements and conclusion; graph rubrics separate plotting, curve and read-off credit.
