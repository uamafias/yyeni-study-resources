## ITEM-6117-9-011-DEF — DEF

AO A; Bloom Remember; 1 mark(s).

State whether most metals in ores are present as uncombined elements or chemically combined with other elements.

**Answer:** Most occur chemically combined with other elements, in compounds.

**Marking:**

- Credit the fact that most occur as compounds (1 mark); accept chemically combined with other elements. Do not require every metal to occur this way.

## ITEM-6117-9-008-MECH — MECH

AO A; Bloom Understand; 1 mark(s).

Explain why magnesium can displace copper from aqueous copper(II) sulfate.

**Answer:** Magnesium is more reactive than copper, so magnesium forms ions while copper ions are converted to copper metal.

**Marking:**

- Credit the causal reason that magnesium is more reactive than copper and therefore displaces it (1 mark); a balanced equation is not required.

## ITEM-6117-9-002-APP — APP

Suggest which material is more suitable for a bendable electrical contact, giving a reason. Material X conducts electricity and bends without breaking. Material Y does not conduct electricity and breaks when bent.

**Answer:** X, because its electrical conductivity and ability to bend meet the stated requirements.

**Marking:**

- Credit X with a reason linked to the given case: it conducts and can be bent into shape (1 mark for the supported choice). Do not award a separate reason mark.

## ITEM-6117-9-021-PROC — PROC

AO A; Bloom Understand; 2 mark(s).

Describe the two reactions by which carbon monoxide is formed from coke and the incoming air in a blast furnace.

**Answer:** Coke burns in oxygen to form carbon dioxide: C + O₂ → CO₂. Carbon dioxide then reacts with more hot coke to form carbon monoxide: CO₂ + C → 2CO.

**Marking:**

- Credit the first step: carbon reacts with oxygen to produce carbon dioxide (1 mark).
- Credit the subsequent reaction/result: carbon dioxide reacts with hot carbon to produce carbon monoxide (1 mark). Accept correct word or balanced symbol equations.

## ITEM-6117-7-062-MISCON — MISCON

AO B; Bloom Apply; 1 mark(s).

Explain why this proposed method is mistaken: after an insoluble salt forms as a precipitate, a learner filters the mixture and evaporates the filtrate to recover that salt.

**Answer:** The insoluble salt is the residue on the filter paper, so the filtrate does not contain that precipitate to recover by evaporation.

**Marking:**

- Credit correction of the confusion: the wanted precipitate is retained as the residue, because it is insoluble (1 mark). This diagnostic distinguishes filtering an insoluble salt from crystallising a dissolved salt.

## ITEM-6117-EX-1 — CALC

AO B; Bloom Apply; 2 mark(s).

Calculate the amount of solute in 32.0 cm³ of a 0.125 mol/dm³ solution. Show your working.

**Answer:** V = 0.0320 dm³; n = c × V = 0.125 × 0.0320 = 0.00400 mol.

**Marking:**

- Equation and substitution with compatible units:  n = c × V = 0.125 × 0.0320 = 0.00400 mol. (1 mark).
- Correct numerical answer and unit:  0.00400 mol. (1 mark).

## ITEM-6117-EX-2 — CALC

AO B; Bloom Apply; 2 mark(s).

Calculate the concentration in mol/dm³ of a solution containing 0.00900 mol in 30.0 cm³. Show your working.

**Answer:** c = n / V = 0.00900 / 0.0300 = 0.300 mol/dm³.

**Marking:**

- Equation and substitution with compatible units: c = n / V = 0.00900 / 0.0300 = 0.300 mol/dm³. (1 mark).
- Correct numerical answer and unit:  0.300 mol/dm³. (1 mark).
