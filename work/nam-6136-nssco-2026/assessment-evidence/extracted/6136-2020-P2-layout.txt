(e) Study Fig. 4 which is an article about foreign investment.

                Foreign investment inflows improve for the second straight
                            quarter as mining sector recovers
                                  by Donald Matthys‌‌ │ Namibian Economist.

 Namibia recorded a much larger net FDI inflow of N$3.31 billion in the third quarter
 of 2017, thanks to an increase in reinvested earnings and foreign investment in
 debt instruments.  During the period under review, Namibia’s FDI stock was mainly
 concentrated in the following sectors:  financial intermediation (50.4%), mining and
 quarrying (27.7%), fishing and fish processing (5.4%) and wholesale and retail trade
 (5.2%).  The central bank highlights that the majority of the FDI stock in the financial
 intermediation and retail sectors originated from South Africa, while investments in
 the mining and quarrying sector stemmed mainly from Mauritius and China, and FDI
 in the fishing and fish processing sector from Spain.
 (Adapted from: Matthys, D. (2018) Foreign Investment Inflows Improve for the Second Straight Quarter as Mining Sector
 Recovers. Namibia Economist. 18 January 2018. Available from https://economist.com.na/32036/markets/foreign-investment-
 inflow-improve-for-the-second-straight-quarter-as-mining-sector-recovers/. Used by permission of Namibia Economist.)

                                                                                              (The Namibian, 18 Jan 2018)
