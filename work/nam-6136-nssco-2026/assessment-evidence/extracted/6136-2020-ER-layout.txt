General comments
The overall performance of the candidates was not satisfactory. This is because most candidates could not interpret
terminologies used and had difficulties in mastering the subject content. Most candidates could not comprehend the
relevance of the sources and instead used their own understanding on the question without referring to the source as
the question required.

The use of sources should be emphasised during the teaching and learning process, at the same time focus should
also be given to the levels of learning, e.g. teaching candidates how to answer questions that require them to describe,
explain, etc.
It was observed that candidates do not read instructions, they could not start each question on a separate page and they
wrote between margins which made it impossible for the markers to indicate marks.

In addition, candidates could not number their answers correctly or chronologically rather they used figures and page
numbers, this should be discouraged. Candidates should be encouraged to separate their work and ensure that they
write neatly and legibly.

Positive Suggestions to Teachers
•   Teach candidates to read instructions and questions carefully before they attempt to answer questions.
•   Teachers should teach candidates to write each question on a separate clean page and number questions correctly.
•   Teach candidates the terminologies and command words that are used in the question paper as listed in the
    syllabus.
•   Teachers should study the examiners report together with their candidates to help them understand what is required
    from them.
•   Teachers should encourage candidates to write neatly and clearly by doing practice during the course of the year.
•   Put more emphasis on interpretation of graphs, diagrams and tables.
•   Teachers should emphasise that candidates must consider number of marks given for each question as most
    candidates do not adhere to these very important sections of the question paper. Candidates either write less than
    the marks allocated or write more than the marks allocated to the question.
•   Teachers should encourage their candidates to study the preparation for examination to enable them to recall
    common core facts.

Positive suggestions
Teachers should cover the syllabus and study examiners’ report yearly. Teachers are advised to start teaching research
in the first trimester of the grade 11 academic year and drill the candidates on different topics.
Teachers must apply the following:

•   teach them how to define terms and not to repeat terms in their definitions e.g. observation is when you observe
•   introduce candidates to different research topics
•   make a clear distinction between research questions and hypothesis
•   focus on teaching candidates on how to formulate research questions in which they address the causes, effects
    and solutions.
•   Teach the candidates on different data collection methods and how to use them.
•   Explain to the candidates what a pilot study is and why researchers need to use a pilot study.
•   Differentiate between primary and secondary data.
•   Teach them what is a sample and how to apply different types of sampling methods.
•   Teachers must expose the candidates to different types of data presentation methods such as pie chart, bar graph,
    table, tally sheet, segmented graph and how to analyse and present them.
