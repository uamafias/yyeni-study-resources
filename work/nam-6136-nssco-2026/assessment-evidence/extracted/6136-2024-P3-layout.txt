1   Study Fig.1, which is about the factors that lead to school drop out in Namibia.
    There are many reasons why learners drop out of school. To understand the
    reasons why learners decide to drop out of school researchers first studied reports
    from the Education Department.  The findings showed that the Kavango, Kunene
    and Omaheke regions had the highest dropout rates in Namibia.

    In these three regions the researchers randomly selected 20% of the schools,
    where they interviewed principals, counsellors, and teachers. The sample of 73
    schools consisted of 58 in the Kavango region, 10 in the Kunene region and 5 in
    the Omaheke region. The researchers also gave questionnaires to the learners
    who had dropped out at some point and later returned to school.

    The study found that, for all learners given questionnaires, 50% dropped out due to
    pregnancy, 20% dropped out due to economic factors, 15% due to system factors,
    11% due to lack of parental involvement, and 4% due to cultural factors.
