1 Study Fig. 1, which is a survey on baby dumping in two regions in Namibia.
The survey was conducted in 2018 using a questionnaire, asking the public why baby
dumping is such a problem in Ohangwena and Oshana regions in Namibia and how
best the public think government can address this problem. A total of 3 742 people
were selected randomly to give their contributions to the questions.
According to the survey, the main reasons were fathers denying paternity, mothers
who were still going to school, mothers that are unaware of foster care, adoption and
institutional care.
