## Papers in this route

**Paper 1 — Reading.** 80 marks, 120 minutes, **50%** of the qualification (syllabus, page 8). Three compulsory questions on three texts. Text A and Text B 700-750 words, Text C 500-650 words. Dictionaries may not be used.

**Paper 2 — Directed Writing and Composition.** 80 marks, 120 minutes, **50%** of the qualification (syllabus, page 8). Two questions, one from each section. Dictionaries may not be used.
