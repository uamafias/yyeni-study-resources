POSITIVE SUGGESTIONS TO TEACHERS
Teach learners the meaning and use of command words as described in the syllabus.

Pay attention to demand and supply diagrams and the effects of changes on the curves.
Teach learners how to apply daily economic news on topics in the syllabus.

Learners must be able to apply general economic knowledge in application of answers.
Use newspapers and articles to broaden the general knowledge of economics learners

Do not stick to textbook knowledge, use other resources to broaden knowledge e.g. internet, YouTube, www.
xtremepapers.com, etc.
