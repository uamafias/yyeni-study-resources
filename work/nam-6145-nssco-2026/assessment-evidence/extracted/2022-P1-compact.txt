Namibian inflation is largely determined by three categories in the Namibian
Consumer Price Index (NCPI) and the Namibian Retail Price Index (NRPI):
(a) Housing, water, electricity, gas and other fuels, (b) Food and non-alcoholic
beverages and (c) Transport. This cumulatively makes up just under 60 percent
of the total inflation basket.
