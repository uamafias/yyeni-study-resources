SUGGESTIONS TO TEACHERS
Teachers must make use of the examiner’s reports to cover the whole syllabus on time.

Teachers are still encouraged to consult various credible sources of information to enrich their subject knowledge.
Teachers must give practical examples in real life situations during lesson presentation. They could use newspapers,
economic magazines and articles in class.

Teachers must expose learners to various economic concepts.

POSITIVE SUGGESTIONS TO TEACHERS
Teachers should use a variety of resources. Teachers should not just use or focus on one textbook; they should make
use of other resources such as YouTube, past question papers and read the examiner’s reports.

Teachers should put emphasis on the analysis of the graph, especially on the change in price and change in quantity
supplied.
Teachers should teach and emphasise the chain of reasoning in class discussions.

Teachers should come up with debate topics to develop critical thinking and discourage learners from giving points
without developments.
Use real life situation, happening in the economy or in Namibia for to relate. Expose learners to the trending news
on NBC and any other related economic affairs. Learners should read newspaper articles (business) to expand their
vocabulary and teachers should assess learners based on the articles read.

Teachers should encourage discussions in class, as well as make use of local printing media in class discussions.
Teachers should teach the correct definition, by focusing on the key words of the definitions.

Teachers should emphasise the command words, such as distinguish, assess, analyse, discuss, and outline to train

Teachers should teach learners the proper way to distinguish by using the connecting word like while in a sentence
such as most public limited companies aim to make a profit, while public cooperations aim to provide essential goods
and services.

Learners should only tabulate when asked to do so.
Teachers should complete the syllabus on time in order to have ample time to complete revision with learners.
Teachers should complete the syllabus by the end of the first term in Grade 11.

Teachers are encouraged to use the prescribed textbook(s), by ensuring that the information is correct and covering
the syllabus. If parts are wrong or not covered in the syllabus topics, then they should use other sources such as old
textbooks.
Teachers should increase the volume of written assessment work in class.

Teachers must teach the syllabus and equip themselves with various credible information to enrich their subject
knowledge.
