# 6145 Economics — business organisation source check

Checked 28 October 2026. General economic comparisons are teaching synthesis. Namibia entity details were checked against primary BIPA material:

- [Close corporation](https://www.bipa.na/business-registration/close-corporation/): legal entity and members’ limited liability.
- [Private companies](https://www.bipa.na/business-registration/companies/private/): restricted share ownership, no public trading, and limited liability.

No registration fees, shareholder thresholds, statutory sections or procedural legal advice are taught. Limited liability is distinguished from risk-free investment and personal guarantees. The geographic meaning of multinational does not imply any single legal form.
