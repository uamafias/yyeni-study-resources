## 1. Target, facts, expected response

- **Slot:** OBJ-6145-3.3-05, under OBJ-6145-3.3-01 ("define price elasticity of demand and supply", syllabus p. 10). PROC, AO B, Apply, Use, 4 marks, difficulty 1. The guidance must name diagram, labels, original and change.
- **Work-order rule:** draw two supply curves with different responsiveness at a declared reference point. Verify the elasticities from the data. A line through the origin is unit elastic. Put limiting cases in the notes, not on the card.
- **Given:** two chair workshops, each supplying 50 a month at N$400. The price rises to N$600. A, which has unused machines, then supplies 90; B, at full capacity, supplies 60. Use initial values.
- **Expected marks:**
  1. Both curves through (50, N$400) on common axes.
  2. Movement along each curve to (90, N$600) and (60, N$600).
  3. A: PES 1.6, elastic.
  4. B: PES 0.4, inelastic.
