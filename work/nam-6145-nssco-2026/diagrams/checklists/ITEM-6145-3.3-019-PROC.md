- **Given (prompt and question table):** maize meal and one brand of crisps, each 100 packets a week at N$20. Both prices rise to N$30. Sales become 75 and 25. Elasticity is calculated from the initial values.

  3. Maize meal PED −0.5, inelastic.
  4. Crisps PED −1.5, elastic.
