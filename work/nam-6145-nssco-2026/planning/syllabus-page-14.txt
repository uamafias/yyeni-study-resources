THEME 7:  MAIN ECONOMIC INDICATORS 
TOPIC GENERAL OBJECTIVES 
Learners will: 
SPECIFIC OBJECTIVES 
Learners should be able to: 
7.1 Prices 
 
 acquire knowledge and understanding of the 
importance of economic indicators in an 
economy (recent changes and current trends) 
 define retail price index and show its simple calculation 
 describe different types of inflation 
 discuss the consequences of inflation 
7.2 Employment  acquire knowledge of aspects relating to 
employment and unemployment 
 describe and discuss the changing patterns and levels 
of employment 
 investigate the reasons for unemployment 
 investigate the consequences of unemployment 
 investigate remedies to unemployment 
7.3 Output  acquire knowledge on how to measure output 
and the reasons for measuring output 
 define Gross Domestic Product, Gross National Product 
and National Income 
 describe the different methods of calculating the 
national income 
 explain reasons for measuring the national income 
7.4 International trade  acquire knowledge of the importance of 
international trade 
 describe the changing patterns of exports and imports 
and the effects on the balance of payments 
 discuss exchange rates and give reasons for their 
fluctuations 
 demonstrate the simple application of absolute and 
comparative advantage in trade
