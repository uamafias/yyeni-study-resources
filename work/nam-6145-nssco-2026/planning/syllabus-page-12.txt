THEME 5:  PRODUCTION 
TOPIC GENERAL OBJECTIVES 
Learners will: 
SPECIFIC OBJECTIVES 
Learners should be able to: 
5.1 The private firm as producer 
and employer 
 be familiar with the aims of firms 
 
 describe and analyse the principle of profit 
maximisation as a goal 
  demonstrate understanding of production 
 
 define production 
 describe what determines the demand for factors of 
production 
 analyse particular situations to show the effects of 
substituting one factor for another  
5.2 Productivity 
 
 be familiar with the importance of productivity 
as a way of measuring performance 
 
 explain the meaning of productivity 
 show how productivity is measured 
 discuss how productivity can be improved 
 explain the effect of Information Technology on 
productivity 
 discuss the effects of HIV and AIDS on productivity 
5.3 Production costs and 
revenue 
 
 acquire knowledge and skill in determining the 
costs of and revenue from production 
 
 define total, average, fixed and variable costs 
 define total and average revenue 
 discuss how costs and revenue might change as output 
changes 
 explain the effect of changes in technology on 
production 
5.4 Economies and 
diseconomies of scale 
 
 demonstrate an understanding of integration, 
economies and diseconomies of scale 
 define and identify the different types of integration 
 explain and illustrate the various economies of scale 
 explain and illustrate the various diseconomies of scale 
 discuss how the economies and diseconomies of scale 
affect business costs 
 discuss the main reasons for the different sizes of firms 
(size of market;  capital;  organisation), and the different 
factors that influence the size of a firm
