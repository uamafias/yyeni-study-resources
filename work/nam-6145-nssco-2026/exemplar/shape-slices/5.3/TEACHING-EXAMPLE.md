# Worked teaching explanation — 5.3

Read after attempting the separate learner items. This worked version deliberately explains the results.

These are invented weekly figures for the fictional Etosha Cloth Workshop in Namibia. Fixed cost is N$120 and total variable cost N$180 for 30 cloth bags. Total cost is N$120 + N$180 = N$300 per week. Average cost is N$300 divided by 30 = N$10 per bag. Fixed cost does not vary with output within the stated short-run range; average fixed cost can still change. Do not add an average cost to a total cost. If 30 bags sell at N$14 each, total revenue is N$420 and average revenue is N$14 per bag. Profit is N$420 minus N$300 = N$120 for the week, using the specified cost basis. Distinguish total values per period from average values per unit; an average-cost decrease need not imply lower total cost.
