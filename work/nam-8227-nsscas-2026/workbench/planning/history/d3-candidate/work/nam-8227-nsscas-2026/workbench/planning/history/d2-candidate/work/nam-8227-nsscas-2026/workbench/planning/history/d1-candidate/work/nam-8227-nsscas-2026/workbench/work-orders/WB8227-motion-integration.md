## Branches, stems and capacities

**Polynomial chain, 6 marks, Apply 3.** Supply acceleration $a(t)=2At+B$ in m/s², initial velocity u in m/s and origin s(0)=0. Ask (velocity) “Find v in terms of t, showing your integration and use of the initial condition”, then (distance) “Find the distance travelled in the first T seconds, in metres, showing your working. Give an exact answer or a value correct to 3 significant figures.” A∈{1,2,3}, B∈{0,1,2}, u∈{1,2,3,4}, T∈{1,2,3,4}; exclude the official tuple (3,1,2,3). **143 parameter tasks**, **36 velocity contexts**, **31 amplitude-normalised model shapes**. Canonical $v=At^2+Bt+u>0$; $d=AT^3/3+BT^2/2+uT$.

Changing only T repeats the first part. No-repeat keys: velocity (A,B,u), distance (A,B,u,T); wholly fresh multipart upper bound 36, not 143. Common positive coefficient rescaling is value variation, not new reasoning. All 143 tasks are feasible. B=0 cases cannot diagnose omission of that zero term; the zero lower primitive means numeric answers cannot diagnose an omitted zero evaluation. These are explicitly non-diagnostic for those isolated errors, with visible working still required. Omission of the nonzero initial velocity is diagnostic throughout.

**Exponential positive, 5 marks, Apply 3.** Supply $v(t)=Ve^{-kt}$, in m/s, and the time interval. Ask (acceleration) for a(t), showing differentiation, then (distance) for travelled length in the first T seconds, in metres, showing integration and both boundary conditions; exact or 3 s.f. V∈{6,12,18,24,30}, k∈{1/4,1/2,1/3}, T∈{1,2,3,4}; exclude official (30,1/2,2). **59 parameter tasks**, **15 acceleration contexts**, **3 amplitude-normalised exponential shapes**, **12 shape/interval tasks**. No k=1, so the chain factor cannot disappear. Canonical $a=-kVe^{-kt}$; $d=(V/k)(1-e^{-kT})$. Parts share a stem, but neither requires the other's answer. No-repeat keys (V,k) and (V,k,T); wholly fresh multipart upper bound 15.

**Interior reversal, 6 marks, Analyse 4.** Supply the expanded quadratic velocity $v(t)=k(t-r)(t+c)$ on 0≤t≤T in m/s, positive direction stated. Render the **expanded polynomial**, not the factored form used here to define its domain. Ask **distance first**: “Find the total distance travelled over this interval, in metres. Show and justify your working.” Then ask signed displacement in metres, with working. Do not name a reversal, supply a root, or ask rest time beforehand. k∈{−3,−2,−1,1,2,3}, r,c,tail∈{1,2,3}, T=r+tail; **r≠tail**. One simple positive zero r lies strictly inside, −c is outside. A cubic primitive is required; triangles are an invalid method for quadratic velocity.

Of 162 starting tuples, **54 r=tail tuples are excluded** because the incorrect straight-line triangle calculation accidentally gives the right total. **108 admitted tasks**, **18 shapes after common amplitude/direction scaling**, two direction orders (54 each). Primitive $F(t)=k[t^3/3+(c-r)t^2/2-rct]$. Distance $|F(r)|+|F(T)-F(r)|$; displacement F(T). Both part-context keys are (k,r,c,tail); wholly fresh upper bound 108. Outcomes and coefficient rescaling do not create additional problem types.
