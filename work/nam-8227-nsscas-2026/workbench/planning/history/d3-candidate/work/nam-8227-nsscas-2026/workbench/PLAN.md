## Evidence-to-design method and review limits

For each selected demand the planner searched accepted item-level assessed/component **union links** (R5-03), then inspected every member of each adopted/compared family to choose its best response/subpart. Native-page manifests preserve all-member lists, hashes, read dates and known unviewed union leads: `planning/visual-source-reads.json/.md`, `calculus-source-reads.json/.md` and `trig-model-source-reads.json/.md`. Unviewed discovery leads are not rejections or proof of absence.

Same-type and structural-branch counts are lower bounds (R6-03/R7-05). Roughly one sampled rejection in five or six was related; these small samples do not estimate a population rate. A positive relation is not the sole design input. Family `distinction_basis`/`units_and_precision`, templated rejection reasons and damaged extracted notes are not design evidence. Native pages and award tables decide the specification. No accepted item-level link is retagged.

Every proposed same-type reference is compared per branch and matched part (R2-02): direct-demand set, response form, givens and scaffold/dependency structure. A whole card never inherits every reference of its family or a sibling branch. Vector part (a) now directly includes addition/subtraction O02:D01 and removes uncredited O02:D03; its equal-demand, form/givens/scaffold comparison permits the part-qualified source label. Its angle part has its own reason. The implicit normal extension does not acquire the gradient-only question’s whole-card badge. Syllabus-derived branches can have no learner exam label. The equality/hold/source checks are recorded in `checks/plan-author-checks.json`.

Sources and quoted originals retain board, code, year, session/variant, question/part and printed/PDF page. NSSCAS and Cambridge originals may be quoted with attribution under Vitalis’s instruction; Cambridge is never labelled Namibian. Textbook originals and images remain internal (`learner_display:false`). Paper pilot examples and diagrams are newly authored. Held 2022 P1 Q13, 2023 P1 Q1(b) and 2023 P1 Q9 originals remain unavailable as learner anchors.
