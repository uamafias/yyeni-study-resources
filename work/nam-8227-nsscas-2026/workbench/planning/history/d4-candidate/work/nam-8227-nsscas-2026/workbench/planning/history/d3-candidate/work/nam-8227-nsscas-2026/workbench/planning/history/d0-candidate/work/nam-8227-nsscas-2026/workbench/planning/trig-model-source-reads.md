## What was learned from the pages

- R-form conversion must respect actual coefficient signs, requested sine/cosine form and phase interval. The pilot keeps one frequency and a complete two-root set, with negative and zero right sides admitted.
- Principal inverse sine requires its output range before forward sine. The defective Namibian original remains held; the simple Cambridge equation supplies valid comparable evidence. Cosine/tangent and nonlinear-inner variants are deferred explicitly.
- A graph labelled ln y has transformed ordinates, and its intercept can contain a model parameter in addition to ln A. The log-base gradient must be inverted in its stated base; source2025 commentary and awards demonstrate that distinction.
- Textbook answer/method errors were not copied; source page reading, not family descriptors, fixed the proposed design.

The family/page search and all15 exemplar calculations are planning evidence only. Runtime generation, fresh-instance coverage, learner diagram output, grading reliability and deployment remain untested.
