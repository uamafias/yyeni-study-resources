# Textbook analysis completion method — 8227

Repair round 2, 16 November 2026. Author proposal for cross-vendor re-review;
this record does not claim the whole textbook is analysed. Current holdings have
663 OCR pages, 572 pages with heading leads and 19 scoped samples. OCR and chapter
breadth are discovery measures, not exercise or objective coverage measures.

1. Establish the population from rendered contents and chapter pages: enumerate
   every Example, Try Now, revision/exam-style set, numbered exercise, multipart
   item, figure and answer-key locator. Preserve the book's actual numbering,
   including section-number inconsistencies. Record unreadable/missing pages.
2. Read every revision/exam-style item and every diagram, graph, construction or
   visual-response item. Review all items addressing unobserved or thin syllabus
   demands. Map each directly assessed demand, prerequisites and response format;
   record in-scope, partial scope or exclusion against 8227 and LIMIT01–08.
3. In routine exercise sets, first classify representative, boundary and structurally
   different items. An ordinary number change is a variation, not a new type. A
   changed learner decision, representation, evidence or connected-part dependency
   can warrant a branch or distinct type. Keep the unclassified population visible.
4. A routine-set sample may stop only after two consecutive reviewed sections add
   no new decision, representation or marking requirement. This stop rule never
   waives item-by-item review of exam-style sets, visual items or gap/thin demands.
   Record inspected item IDs and the exact reason for stopping.
5. For every item used as design evidence, inspect the actual answer/worked method,
   independently recompute a representative result and check diagram consistency.
   Locate answer keys rather than assuming an answer exists. Record errors and
   conflicts with official conventions separately; official assessment guidance
   governs an examination-aligned branch. Textbook reasoning may still be useful.
6. A second vendor independently classifies a seeded 10% sample of classified items,
   plus all disputed distinctions, gaps and source defects. Resolve discrepancies
   and enlarge the sample when disagreement indicates a systematic problem.
7. Completion means the exhaustive populations above are reconciled, every sampled
   or unsampled item has a disposition, demanded gaps remain explicit and the
   independent review accepts the type/branch/variation distinctions. It never
   means that every possible future examination problem has been predicted.
