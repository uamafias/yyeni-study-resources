# Textbook census and verification method — 8227

The census is a page enumeration. It does not inherit completeness from heading counts or prior page-read counts. Each completed page names all question starts, continuations, worked illustrations, prompts and mathematical teaching figures seen on its render. Prose-only pages state that explicitly. Unread pages remain pending.

The working envelope is Goldie PDF15–332 and YM PDF12–585 (PDF139 duplicates printed127 and is recorded separately). Goldie's excluded sections are screened at their actual pages before exclusion is reconfirmed; chapter8 is not excluded wholesale. Source pages outside this envelope and answer pages are recorded separately where relevant. All13 YM chapters are required. Reading the envelope does not automatically make every item in-scope.

A source item is one numbered question, worked example or independently posed unnumbered task. Subparts stay inside it. A continuation is linked to the original item. A teaching figure is a numbered figure, graph, diagram, explanatory table or annotated visual procedure. Ordinary aligned algebra within a worked solution remains part of that example, rather than becoming a separate figure. Context photographs and page decoration are recorded as nonmathematical visuals, not assessed items.

Figure records have a parent item where appropriate and teach-demand links. They have no assessed-demand links merely because they illustrate a demand. This prevents figure counts from inflating assessed textbook support.

The mapping rule separates the learner's explicit requested result, justification, representation or enforced method (assessed demands) from intermediate techniques needed along a valid route (component demands). A skill appearing only in the worked solution is not automatically directly assessed. Where a broad demand contains several aspects, the item states the aspect requested. An unlisted prerequisite is described in words rather than assigned an unrelated demand ID. Every item is re-read under this rule; unprocessed items retain their old candidate status.

Answer verification transcribes the question inputs and printed key separately, derives from the question only, then compares. A located key is not an answer check. Every subpart needs its own status, exact key page or explicit absence, method and date. The executable source-check tools are analysis tools, not runtime question generators or marking implementations. Diagrams and sketches require feature checks on renders in addition to algebra.

Textbook originals remain internal. No author-selected review sample is prepared; the independent reviewer chooses its own blind sample. Step3 waits until this work is complete and independently accepted.
