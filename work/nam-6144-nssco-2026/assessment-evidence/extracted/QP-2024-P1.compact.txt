1 Kapo is the production manager at Delight that manufactures a range of toilet
paper and serviettes. The company uses flow production methods in its factory.
The following data was taken from the financial statements of the company.
Direct material N$22 000
Direct labour: wages N$45 000
Indirect labour: rent paid N$23 000
(a) (i) Define the term unit cost.
(ii) State the difference between income and expenses.
(b) State the difference between fixed cost and variable cost.
(c) Identify and explain how two stakeholders would use the financial
statements of Delight.
--- PDF PAGE 3 ---
(d) Evaluate the appropriateness of job production for Delight products.

2 Beauty (Pty) Ltd provide a wide range of fashion clothes and shoes for boys and
girls between 8 and 15 years. The company operates in a highly competitive
and fast changing market. The management believe that the company is market
orientated. The marketing manager is concerned about the falling sales of boys’
clothes.
(a) (i) Define the term secondary research.
(ii) State two external sources of secondary research.
(b) List two objectives of marketing.
(c) Using examples from Beauty (Pty) Ltd distinguish between a market
orientated and a product orientated business.
--- PDF PAGE 5 ---
(d) Advise Beauty (Pty) Ltd on the most suitable ways to segment its
market.

3 Ewandre is the general manager of Hills Guest House. She believes that
customer service is important. She uses non-financial rewards to motivate her
workers. All workers are members of a trade union. The Namibian government
tries to look after a business workers’ well-being.
(a) (i) Define the term shop steward.
(ii) Identify two types of industrial action.
(b) Define the term redundancy.
(c) Explain how two rewards could be used in Hills Guest House to motivate
workers.
--- PDF PAGE 7 ---
(d) Evaluate methods the government might use to protect employees.

4 Auto Suppliers Ltd is a large business in Namibia whose management are
looking for a country where they might relocate their vehicle factory. The financial
manager informs the management about the following problems.
- Difficulties with raw material and labour force
- Rising rent and taxes
- Production possibilities in Botswana
The Namibian government control business activity by using two policies to limit
economic fluctuation.
(a) (i) Describe the type of economy of Namibia.
(ii) Identify two advantages of a market economy.
(b) State two main methods of measuring the size of a business.
--- PDF PAGE 9 ---
(c) Explain the role of the government by using two economic policies to
control the economy.
--- PDF PAGE 10 ---
(d) Assess whether Auto Suppliers Ltd should relocate production to a country
outside of Namibia.

5 Sporty Dealers is a large business providing sport organising services and
equipment in Namibia and South Africa. The Marketing manager is concerned
about their informative advertising which have an effect on the price of sport
equipment. Sporty Dealers hired a local business who is involved in clean-up
projects of sport fields after sport events. The local business clean-up price
is determined by the amount of pollution in the form of paper, glass, cans and
plastic that are thrown around during a sport event.
(a) (i) Define the term distribution.
(ii) Name two elements of the marketing mix.
(b) Identify two factors that will increase business productivity.
--- PDF PAGE 12 ---
(c) Using examples from Sporty Dealers distinguish between informative and
persuasive advertising.
--- PDF PAGE 13 ---
(d) Suggest the most appropriate way the government can protect the
environment where Sporty Dealers operates.
