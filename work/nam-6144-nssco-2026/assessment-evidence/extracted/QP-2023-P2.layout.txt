Nam Charcoal

Juan	     and	     Rudi	     started	     a	     partnership	     five	     years	     ago,	     Nam	     Charcoal,	     a	     business	     that
produces	charcoal	for	the	local	market.		Their	business	is	situated	in	the	north	of	Namibia.

The	  business	  expanded	  quickly	  and	  they	  decided	  to	  relocate	  the	  main	  office	  and	  some
production	to	Botswana,	because	they	export	6	000	ton	of	charcoal	to	Botswana	each	year.
Resources	are	easily	available	in	Botswana	and	no	minimum	wage	is	paid	to	workers.

The	  expansion	  of	  the	  business	  changed	  the	  organisational	  structure	  of	  the	  partnership.
They	needed	to	appoint	area	managers	for	each	country	as	well	as	a	financial	manager,	a
marketing	manager,	a	production	manager	and	more	workers	in	the	production	department
for	the	expanded	business.		The	partners	borrowed	money	from	a	local	Namibian	bank	to
finance	the	expansion.

Juan	and	Rudi	asked	their	legal	advisor	for	advice	on	whether	they	should	change	to	a	close
corporation.	  	  He	  discussed	  the	  pros	  and	  cons	  of	  each	  form	  of	  business	  ownership	  with
them.

Juan	and	Rudi	know	that	with	the	expansion	to	Botswana	they	must	consider	their	pricing
strategy	for	charcoal	in	Botswana.		The	market	is	very	competitive	in	Botswana	for	selling
charcoal.		The	Namibian	government	recently	announced	a	minimum	wage	in	the	charcoal
industry.

In	   the	   first	   year,	   2019,	   they	   produced	   5	   000	   tons	   of	   charcoal.	   	   From	   2020	   −	   2022	   the
production	increased	with	1	500	tons	per	year	in	Namibia	and	Botswana.

The	following	information	is	available	from	Nam	Charcoal.
