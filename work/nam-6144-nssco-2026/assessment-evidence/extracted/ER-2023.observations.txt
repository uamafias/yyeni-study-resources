3. POSITIVE SUGGESTIONS TO TEACHERS
Teachers should teach learners according to syllabus and not just using a textbook – teach basic competencies.
Teachers should encourage learners through exercises with cases so as to know how to put their answers in 
context.
Teachers have to teach learners on how to answer evaluation questions.  Evaluation is only marked when the 
answer is two sided and learners should clearly indicate their choice.
Learners should be exposed to different synonyms such as advantages = benefits and disadvantages = costs.
