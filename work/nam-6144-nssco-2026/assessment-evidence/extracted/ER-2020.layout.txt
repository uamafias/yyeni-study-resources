Application is answering in the context of the case/question.  If the question is about an airline, then capacity utilisation
in terms of seats on flights, fuel, costs, in-flight meals, etc. may be appropriate to gain reward for application, unless they
are specifically mentioned in the case/question.

The use of information in the case or question, such as names of companies and/or products is not application.

GENERAL COMMENTS
Learners ignore application totally.  (A loss of 20 marks in total)
Question (d) must be answered in comparison to receive evaluation marks.
Analysis no longer only requires a further explanation, its impact must also to be explored.
Learners were not taught how to answer questions.  Teachers must explain carefully how learners should go about
answering questions by considering knowledge, analysis and applications.
In general, learners do not have enough knowledge to score high marks and without knowledge a learner cannot obtain
marks for analysis, application and evaluation.
No knowledge point, no marks.

POSITIVE SUGGESTIONS TO TEACHERS
-    Teachers should take the new syllabus for Business Studies Grade 11 into consideration and not just a textbook –
     to make sure that all topics are covered.
-    Learners should be coached on how to answer a question using knowledge, analysis, application and evaluation.
     Learners cannot score marks without showing knowledge.
-    Teachers and learners should place more emphasis on the accounting functions especially for learners who don’t
     have an Accounting and Business Studies combination.
-    Give    regular    assessments    based    on    revised    syllabus    pro-forma.        Learners    should    read    the    questions    and    give
     answers in full and not only in part.  Learners should not give answers in general.
-    Teachers should seek assistance when they are challenged and need help.
