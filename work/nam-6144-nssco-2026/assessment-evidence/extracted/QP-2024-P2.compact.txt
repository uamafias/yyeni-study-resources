PENSAK
Andrew is a sole trader and owns a business called Pensak. The business specialises
in making special designed pencil bags/pouches, marker cases and pencil cases. The
business has grown quickly, but profits stayed the same over the last two years. A
sudden increase in demand in 2024 forces Andrew to change the working hours which
put the workers under great pressure and each worker gets now safety instruction that
is applicable in the factory. Due to the growth, there was a need for a more formal
organisational structure. Andrew make use of worker councils and quality circles as a way
of worker participation to motivate his workforce.
Andrew has ten skilled workers which consist of two store workers, three shop workers, one
cutter, three women who stitch the pencil bags on sewing machines, as well as a receptionist.
Workers are paid by piece rate but they didn’t like this method. Effective communication
and safety of each worker is vital for the image and efficiency of Pensak.
Andrew decide to give his workforce more responsibly, recognition for their good work,
promotion and growth opportunities in the work place. This will lead to a more loyal
workforce.
Andrew wants to expand the business and decided to form a partnership with his
brother Flip.
Currently the pencil bags are promoted through advertising. Andrew is considering
personal selling and public relations to make schools more aware of his products.
The Namibian government plays a vital role in the economy and this has an influence on
Pensak’s business activities especially the customers.
The following information was taken from the balance sheet of Pensak in 2024.
Current assets N$53 000
Current liabilities N$57 000
Fixed assets N$104 000
Long-term liabilities N$246 000
In 2023 the result of the solvency position was 0,98 : 1.
