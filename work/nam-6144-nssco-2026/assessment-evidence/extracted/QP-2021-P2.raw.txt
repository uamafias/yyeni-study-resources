BG	Chem	(Pty)	Ltd	is	a	business	that	produces,	sanitisers,	disinfectants	and	Personal		
Protective	Equipment	(PPE).		It	also	helps	with	disinfecting	houses,	shops	and	schools.
At	first,	the	business	used	a	batch	production	method.		 Towards	the	end	of	2020,	the		
business	lost	its	market	share.		As	BG	Chem	(Pty)	Ltd	could	not	keep	up	with	demand,	 	
local	customers	started	buying	disinfectants	and	PPE	from	its	rivals.		The	owners	of	BG	
Chem	(Pty)	Ltd	decided	to	purchase	modern	machinery	that	can	be	used	to	produce	disin-
fectants	and	PPEs	in	large	quantities.
At	the	beginning	of	2021,	the	owners	thought	of	expanding	to	benefit	from	cost	advantages.	 	
They	decided	to	convert	the	business	into	a	Private	Limited	Company.
Workers	 decided	 to	 communicate	 their	 concerns	 about	 a	 range	 of	 problems	 to	 the		
management.	 	However,	 every	 time	 they	 tried	 to	 raise	 these	 problems,	 employers		
promised	it	would	be	discussed,	with	feedback	provided	through	emails	-	this	never		
happened.		Some	workers	also	felt	it	was	not	a	good	idea	for	the	management	to	respond	
via	email	as	most	of	them	would	end	up	not	getting	this	information.		The	management	
decided	to	involve	the	workers	through	quality	circles	and	a	democratic	style	of	leadership	
as	ways	to	improve	worker	participation.
Discussions	between	managers	and	trade	unions	broke	down.		The	trade	union	decided	to	
take	industrial	action.
