1 Claude and Mary are the owners of Glamour Cosmetics (GC). It is a leading
supplier of cosmetics in Namibia. GC has a diverse workforce coming from a
wide range of backgrounds. The Human Resource manager has focused on
recruiting a talented management and offer an appropriate reward system to
them. Claude and Mary try to attract and retain the right people. Some of the
workforce are HIV positive.
(a) (i) State two groups involved in business activity.
(ii) Define effective management.
(b) Identify two functions of management.
(c) Analyse why cooperation and joint responsibility are important in GC.
--- PDF PAGE 3 ---
(d) Suggest how the government can protect people with HIV and Aids in the
workplace at GC. Justify your answer.

2 Joseph has decided to relocate his shoe factory, Vellies for All, from Windhoek
to Outjo. They produce fashionable “vellies” for men and women. He decided
to brand his vellies because branded products give the impression of shoes of
a higher quality. He employs 10 workers, trained on the job, in the factory. He
needs a business plan to obtain finance from the bank.
(a) (i) Identify two needs for funds.
(ii) Define the term business plan.
(b) Use two pieces of information that Joseph should include in his production
plan.

(c) Distinguish between brand name and brand loyalty at Vellies for All.

3 P&A Textiles is a T-shirt manufacturing business. Patric and Aneldi did research
before they started the business. They set certain marketing objectives for
their business. Aneldi decided to segment the market for T-shirts to save on
resources and to respond more precisely to the needs of their customers. Due
to an increase in demand they need to recruit more workers. All workers should
receive training to be more efficient.
(a) (i) Define the term marketing.
(ii) List two marketing objectives.
(b) Define the term training.

4 Quick Smart is a retailer that sells a wide range of fast food products. The price
and packaging are two elements of the marketing mix which are important to the
management. The Namibian government recently announced new health and
safety regulations for employees. All of Quick Smart’s employees have social
security.
(a) (i) Define price.
(ii) State two functions of packaging.
(b) Identify two benefits, to an employee, of social security.
(c) Discuss how the government may protect consumers of Quick Smart.
--- PDF PAGE 13 ---
(d) Evaluate how Quick Smart will be affected by new health and safety
regulations for employees.

5 Dolls Paradise is selling dolls to a niche market. In the past few years,
the demand for these dolls has increased due to effective advertising. The
management did some market research and discovered a gap in the market for
porcelain dolls. There are no competitors in the market for these dolls. Dolls
Paradise uses sales promotion for a short period of time to boost its sales.
(a) (i) Identify two requirements for effective advertising.
(ii) Define the term market research.
(b) Identify two pricing strategies.
(c) Discuss the use of informative and persuasive advertising by Dolls
Paradise.
--- PDF PAGE 15 ---
(d) Suggest suitable methods of sales promotion that Dolls Paradise can use
for porcelain dolls.
