5 (a) Poorly answered.  Most learners couldn’t give the reasons why and how the government assists 
exporting businesses.  A few learners were able to identify the how and why but failed to analyse their 
answers and to include application.
Answer 
Discuss how and why the government may intervene in assisting Suntech (Pty) Ltd. in exporting 
goods and services. [8]
Knowledge (2 x 1) award one mark for why and one mark for how the government may intervene in 
exporting goods and services
Application (2 x 1) award one mark for each how and why with specific reference to Suntech
Analysis (2 x 2) award up to two marks for a correct analysis on how and up to two marks for correct 
analysis on why the government may intervene in exporting goods and services
Relevant points may include:
Why
Suntech laptops (ap) the government will guarantee payment which will encourage Suntech to export.
(b) Fairly answered.  Most learners could consider the social costs and social benefits of allowing the 
new factory to be built but they failed to evaluate and come to a judgement.
Answer 
Assume you are the government agent responsible for planning decisions.  Considering social costs 
and social benefits, would you allow the new factory to be built? [12]
Knowledge (2 x 2) award up to two marks for social benefits and two marks for social costs
Application (2 x 1) award one mark for each benefit and each cost with specific reference to a new factory
Analysis (2 x 1) award up to one mark for analysis of social costs and one mark for social benefits
Evaluation (4) award up to four marks for evaluative analysis of social costs and social benefits
Relevant points may include:
Social costs
