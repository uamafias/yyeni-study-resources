Suntech (Pty) Ltd
Suntech (Pty) Ltd. is a technology company which focuses on producing technical
equipment such as cell phones, tablets, laptops and iPads. It offers a great variety
of products and services to the Namibian market.
The company decided to carry out market research because the industry is competitive.
Results from the research show that there are many local small businesses producing
similar technical equipment.
Suntech (Pty) Ltd. often changes the range of products manufactured and sold.
Therefore, the highly skilled workforce continuously needs to undergo training on the
new production processes that may be required. As the firm has grown, communication
between workers and management has become harder. Worker participation has
been suggested to improve the relationship between the management and workers
of Suntech (Pty) Ltd.
A new range of products will be produced for the export market. Currently the existing
factory does not have the production capacity and a new factory needs to be built.
They have applied to the Local Council for planning approval to build their new factory.
The site chosen for this factory is near a residential area.
The company had high sales over the years. However, during the first quarter of
2020, Suntech (Pty) Ltd. Experienced a decrease in sales that led to huge losses.
The board members suggested selling the company to the government.
The table shows financial information extracted from Suntech’s accounts for the
financial year 2019/2020.

Stock 38 000
Debtors 16 000
Net profit 40 000
Cash 8 000
