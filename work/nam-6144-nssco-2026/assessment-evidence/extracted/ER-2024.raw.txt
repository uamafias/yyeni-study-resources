Identify and explain how two stakeholders would use the 
financial statements of Delight.
Manager (k)
 - The manager will be able to see which products of the company 
are performing satisfactorily and which products or divisions are 
performing poorly to help in decision making/growth of  
business - status. (an)

creditors may refuse to offer additional credit to the company. 
(an)

money from a bank, (an) want to see if they will get their money
