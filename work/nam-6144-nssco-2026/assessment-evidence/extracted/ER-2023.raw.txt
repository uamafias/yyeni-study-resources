3. POSITIVE SUGGESTIONS TO TEACHERS
Teach the basic competency in the syllabus and NOT the textbook.
Expose learners to proper approach to answer (c) and (d) level questions.
Each answer should be awarded knowledge, application and analysis.
Assessment objective “knowledge” should be the point of departure in every lesson topic presentation, without 
the ‘knowledge” learner will score no marks for analysis and application.
Teach learners that their answers must be relevant to that specific case study and not just in general.
Test and examination papers must have the same format as a final examination paper.
HINT: When you start your lesson refer to the specific objective of the day.  Ask learner where this objective 
is on the grid A, B, C, D.  Refer to the command words for the specific objective.  Ask learners what is 
required to answer this objective.   Example: knowledge, analysis, application and evaluation.  How 
many marks are allocated to each of above.
It is advisable that the government section of the syllabus should be taught in grade 10 already as the teachers 
only rush through it to try to complete the syllabus.
