## Plan problems requiring repair

In the standard upward-supply/downward-demand diagram, a wage above equilibrium creates excess supply; elasticity affects the size of the response. Replace the necessary-elasticity condition and regenerate affected work orders/check inputs. Do not change the condition to an unconditional claim about every real labour market.

## Learner reconstruction spot checks

For living standards, the notes now support an 8-mark discussion of whether real GDP per head is sufficient: comparable real output per person is useful, but distribution, under-recording, unpaid services, leisure and environmental damage limit it; HDI adds health and education but still hides inequality and omits other aspects. A judgement should depend on the purpose of the comparison and use multiple indicators. This is supportable from the notes without inventing income data.

For government intervention, the notes support an 8-mark discussion of a price ceiling: a binding ceiling can improve affordability for buyers served but contracts quantity supplied and creates excess demand, with rationing and black-market risks. A non-binding ceiling changes nothing. Effectiveness depends on costs, enforcement, supply support and access for poor households; direct provision or subsidies have funding costs. This is supportable from the notes. An 8-mark task that only recites the diagram does not test this discussion.
