## Content — printed pp.3–18 / PDF pp.7–22

- Theme 1: 1.1 The nature and purpose of business activity; 1.2 Types of business activity.
- Theme 2: 2.1 Forms of business organisations; 2.2 Organisational structures, by level and by function; 2.3 Finance in the private/public sector, meeting targets, raising finance; 2.4 The accounting function, planning and controlling business activity; 2.5 Management of business; 2.6 Communication.
- Theme 3: 3.1 Why work? Needs of the individual; 3.2 Rewards for work; 3.3 Groups and relationships; 3.4 Formal representation and participation; 3.5 Recruitment, training and human resources.
- Theme 4: 4.1 The market and marketing; 4.2 Market research; 4.3 Business plan; 4.4 The marketing mix: product and packaging, price, promotion, distribution; 4.5 Deciding on the scale of operations, and location.
- Theme 5: 5.1 The reasons for regulation of business activity; 5.2 Aiding and influencing the producer; 5.3 Aiding and protecting the employee; 5.4 Persuading and protecting the consumers and providers of capital.
