## Content and response demands

The eleven syllabus topics (pp. 4–5) are Scientific processes; Matter; Materials; Stoichiometry; Electrochemistry; Chemical reactions; Acids, bases and salts; Qualitative analysis; Metals; Organic chemistry; and Environmental and industrial chemistry. The glossary of command terms is on pp. 42–43. It includes Define, State, List, Explain, Describe, Discuss, Outline, Deduce/predict, Comment, Suggest, Find, Calculate, Measure, Determine, Estimate, Sketch, Construct, Compare and Classify, plus the longer “What do you understand by/What is meant by” form. Actual paper usage and tariffs remain to be mined in Step 3.

Paper 3 candidates are not supplied the qualitative-analysis notes in Annexe B (p. 39). Other explicit content limits are captured with source excerpts in `syllabus-facts.json`; they must be turned into the later scope register before authoring.
