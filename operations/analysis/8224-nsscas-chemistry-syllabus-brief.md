# 8224 NSSCAS Chemistry — syllabus brief (step 2)

Source: NIED NSSCAS Chemistry syllabus 8224, updated 20 November 2020. Every figure below is in `work/nam-8224-nsscas-2026/curriculum/syllabus-facts.json` with its printed page, PDF page and verbatim line (143 quotes re-verified against the PDF, 0 failures). Page numbers here are printed pages.

## Qualification
One-year course, about 180 guided learning hours (p. 3). NSSCO Chemistry is recommended prior learning (p. 3). Grades a to e (p. 3). All learners sit Papers 1, 2 and 3 (p. 31).

## Assessment objectives (p. 30; weights p. 32)
| AO | Name | Printed weight | Grid marks P1 / P2 / P3 |
|---|---|---:|---|
| A | Knowledge with understanding (A1–A6; not more than 20% recall) | 40% | 20 / 30 / 0 |
| B | Handling information, application and solving problems (B1–B7) | 40% | 20 / 30 / 0 |
| C | Practical (experimental and investigative) skills and abilities (C1–C4) | 20% | 0 / 0 / 40 |

## Papers (p. 31)
| Paper | Description | Marks | Duration | Printed weight | AO split in the paper |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Theory: multiple choice, 40 four-choice items | 40 | 1 hour | 31% | A 50%, B 50% |
| 2 | Theory: compulsory short-answer, structured and free-response | 60 | 1 hour 15 min | 46% | A 50%, B 50% |
| 3 | Advanced practical skills: two or three experiments, timed | 40 | 2 hours | 23% | C 100% |
Total 140 marks. Qualitative-analysis notes (Annexe D) are supplied with Paper 3 (p. 31). The data booklet is available for Papers 1 and 2 (p. 55).

## Command words (p. 34–35)
The syllabus table has 20 rows: Define; What do you understand by / What is meant by; State; List; Explain; Describe; Discuss; Outline; Deduce / predict; Comment; Suggest; Find; Calculate; Measure; Determine; Estimate; Sketch; Construct; Compare; Classify. ## Content (p. 4–29)
4 themes, 20 topics, 50 sub-topics (52 teachable units: topics 1.4 and 4.5 have no sub-topics).
Theme 1 Particulate nature of matter and stoichiometry (1.1–1.4) · Theme 2 Physical chemistry (2.1–2.4) · Theme 3 Inorganic chemistry (3.1–3.5) · Theme 4 Organic chemistry and analysis (4.1–4.7).

## Stated limits (12, with pages, in `limits`)
Mass spectrometer working (1.1.3), hybridisation (1.3.4), relationship between Kp and Kc (2.3.1), quadratic equations in equilibrium calculations (2.3.1), effect of paired electrons in sub-shells (3.1.1, 3.1.2), E/Z nomenclature and diastereoisomers (4.5), and for Paper 3: systematic qualitative analysis and traditional separation methods, identifying unspecified ions, tests on other substances, and reagent-preparation guidance.
