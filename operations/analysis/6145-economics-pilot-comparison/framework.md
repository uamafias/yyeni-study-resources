## 1. Assessment structure

| Paper | Duration | Marks | Weight | Sections (syllabus p14, lines 519-561) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 h 30 min | 80 | 50% | A: short answer, 40 marks, 'assessed by AO A'; B: longer questions on real situations, 40 marks, 'AO B and C'. All compulsory. |
| 2 | 2 h | 80 | 50% | A: structured question(s), 20 marks, includes drawing and interpreting diagrams, compulsory; B: 3 of 5 structured essay questions at 20 marks (60 marks). |

Observed in all ten papers: P1 = 40 + 40 (P1 Section B in 2020 is Q23 [23] + Q24 [17]; 2021-2024 are 20 + 20; 2024 has two Section B questions). P2 = Q1 [20] compulsory + Q2-Q6 each [20], three to be answered. Papers therefore set 80 (P1) and 120 (P2) marks. Calculators allowed (QP covers). Guided learning about 130 hours over two years (lines 157-162).

### Assessment objectives (p13, lines 471-512; pdf 17)

- **A Knowledge with understanding**: facts, definitions, concepts, principles, theories; economic vocabulary and conventions. Often: define, describe, list, outline, state, identify (line 487-488).
- **B Application and analysis**: use data, recognise patterns and deduce relationships; select, organise, interpret data; analyse in verbal, numerical, diagrammatic, pictorial, graphical form; recognise limits of theory. Often: analyse, calculate, comment, compare, contrast, explain (499-500).
- **C Evaluation and decision making**: distinguish evidence from opinion and make reasoned judgements; evaluate social and environmental implications; make decisions and proposals from novel data. Often: assess, discuss, evaluate, justify (511-512).

### Tensions inside the scheme

- Scheme says P1 Section A is assessed by AO A only (p14), but Section A items in 2020-2024 include calculations (e.g. 2022 Q13, 2024 Q17), reading diagrams (2020 Q8, 2022 Q9-10, 2024 Q9) and 'explain'/'describe' items; the syllabus's own AO text places 'calculate' and 'explain' under B.
- Grid A/B/C percentages cannot be reproduced from the papers without a mark scheme; ER-printed labels (Kn/Ap/An/Ev) are partial and sometimes at odds with the syllabus verb bands (e.g. 2024 P1 Q23(a) 'Describe' labelled Application and analysis; 2022 P2 Q4(b) labelled Kn).
- Some specific-objective bullets contain no content list ('types of markets', 'aims of trade unions', 'factors affecting trade union activities in Namibia'), so adequate response has to be authored from outside sources.

### Topic 1.1 Basic economic problem: scarcity and exercise of choice (heading line 218, printed p4)
General objective (syllabus): learners will understand the nature of the economic problem.

**1.1.1** (topic 1.1, bullet 1, line 218-219): "describe the nature of the economic problem (limited resources and unlimited wants)"
  - Demand: recall/describe (verb: describe). Syllabus definitions: describe = state the points of a topic / give characteristics and main features.
  - Skills: state scarcity as limited resources against unlimited wants; separate needs from wants; recognise that scarcity is about resources, not about goods and services.
  - Pedagogical prerequisites (not syllabus): needs vs wants; meaning of resources.
  - Adequate response: States both halves: resources are limited and wants/needs are unlimited; links the problem to having to choose.
  - Exam emphasis: three or more years; primary marks 7. Primary: 2020-P1-Q1 [1], 2021-P1-Q1 [2], 2021-P1-Q2 [1]~, 2022-P1-Q1 [1], 2024-P1-Q1 [2]. Secondary: none.
  - ER misconceptions: 2020 P1-Q1: lost marks writing "not enough goods and services" instead of resources
  - Formats: FC, MCQ, SAB
**1.1.2** (topic 1.1, bullet 2, line 220): "explain and illustrate opportunity cost"
  - Demand: apply/analyse (verb: explain/illustrate). Syllabus definitions: explain = set out purposes or reasons / make the relationships between things evident / provide why and/or how, and support with relevant evidence.
  - Skills: define opportunity cost as the next best alternative given up; identify the choice made in a scenario; identify the specific forgone next-best option; use the word 'next' / 'best'.
  - Pedagogical prerequisites (not syllabus): 1.1.1 scarcity and choice.
  - Adequate response: Gives the full definition (next best alternative forgone) and names both the chosen option and the specific forgone option from the case.
  - Exam emphasis: two years (primary tag in 2 of 5 years); primary marks 7. Primary: 2020-P2-Q4c [4], 2023-P1-Q1 [3]. Secondary: 2022-P2-Q2b [7], 2024-P1-Q1 [2].
  - ER misconceptions: 2020 P2-Q4c: lost definition marks; used own illustration not extract; 2022 P2-Q2b: key word NEXT missing; could not extract best alternative
  - Formats: FC, SAB, DG
**1.1.3** (topic 1.1, bullet 3, line 221-222): "evaluate the implications of specific courses of action in terms of opportunity cost"
  - Demand: judgement/evaluation (highest verb: evaluate/discuss). Syllabus definitions: evaluate = judge or calculate the quality, importance, amount, or value of something.
  - Skills: compare two or more courses of action; state what is forgone for each; judge consequences for different parties; reach and justify a decision.
  - Pedagogical prerequisites (not syllabus): 1.1.2 opportunity cost.
  - Adequate response: Names the choice and its forgone alternative, weighs gains against losses (with reference to the case) and states a judgement.
  - Exam emphasis: single year only (primary tag in 1 of 5 years); primary marks 7. Primary: 2022-P2-Q2b [7]. Secondary: none.
  - ER misconceptions: 2022 P2-Q2b: key word NEXT missing; could not extract best alternative
  - Formats: EXT, SAB

### Topic 1.2 Types of economic systems and economic resources (heading line 223, printed p4)
General objective (syllabus): learners will demonstrate understanding of economic resources in different economic systems.

**1.2.1** (topic 1.2, bullet 1, line 223-224): "describe the factors of production (land, labour, capital, entrepreneurship)"
  - Demand: recall/describe (verb: describe). Syllabus definitions: describe = state the points of a topic / give characteristics and main features.
  - Skills: name the four factors of production; classify a resource in a scenario as land/labour/capital/entrepreneurship; give the reward of each factor (rent, wages, interest, profit) - examined although not printed in the bullet.
  - Pedagogical prerequisites (not syllabus): 1.1.1 resources.
  - Adequate response: Names all four, matches each to an example from the case, and (if asked) the correct reward.
  - Exam emphasis: three or more years; primary marks 9. Primary: 2020-P1-Q2 [1]~, 2020-P2-Q4a [6], 2022-P1-Q2 [1], 2023-P1-Q3 [1]. Secondary: none.
  - ER misconceptions: 2022 P1-Q2: few gave entrepreneurship
  - Formats: FC, MCQ, SAB
**1.2.2** (topic 1.2, bullet 2, line 225-226): "explain allocation of resources in market, planned and mixed economic systems"
  - Demand: apply/analyse (verb: explain). Syllabus definitions: explain = set out purposes or reasons / make the relationships between things evident / provide why and/or how, and support with relevant evidence.
  - Skills: describe who owns resources and how prices allocate them in a market economy; describe central planning and state ownership in a planned economy; describe the mixed combination; answer what/how/for whom under each system.
  - Pedagogical prerequisites (not syllabus): 1.2.1 factors of production; 3.2.2 price mechanism (for the market case).
  - Adequate response: Describes ownership, decision-maker and role of prices for each of the three systems and distinguishes them.
  - Exam emphasis: three or more years; primary marks 10. Primary: 2020-P1-Q3 [1], 2021-P2-Q2a [6], 2023-P1-Q2 [1], 2024-P2-Q4a [2]. Secondary: none.

  - Formats: FC, SAB
**1.2.3** (topic 1.2, bullet 3, line 227-228): "evaluate the merits and effectiveness of different economic systems"
  - Demand: judgement/evaluation (highest verb: evaluate/discuss). Syllabus definitions: evaluate = judge or calculate the quality, importance, amount, or value of something.
  - Skills: list merits and demerits of each system; apply them to a named economy or case; weigh the points and conclude on effectiveness.
  - Pedagogical prerequisites (not syllabus): 1.2.2 allocation in each system.
  - Adequate response: Balanced merits and limitations, applied to the case, with a justified conclusion (ER notes one-sided and 'mirror' answers lose marks).
  - Exam emphasis: two years (primary tag in 2 of 5 years); primary marks 23. Primary: 2021-P2-Q2b [7], 2021-P2-Q2c [7], 2024-P1-Q2 [2], 2024-P2-Q4c [7]. Secondary: 2020-P1-Q23e [8].
  - ER misconceptions: 2021 P2-Q2c: discussed both systems not merits of mixed system; 2024 P1-Q2: gave features of market system not reasons to leave it; 2024 P2-Q4c: gave characteristics of command and market economy
  - Formats: EXT, SAB
