## Scope and command words

Six topics: Scientific processes; General Physics; Thermal Physics; Properties
of waves; Electricity and magnetism; Nuclear Physics. The outline (printed
pp.4-5 [PDF 7-8]) reconciles with 78 content-table headings, including six topic
headings. The table has 335 specific-objective bullets; these are source bullets,
not decomposed assessable targets or proposed card counts. The glossary has 30
rows, including joint labels, at printed pp.41-42 [PDF 44-45]. Its exact definitions
are transcribed; Step 3 will decide which commands are evidenced in examinations.

Three explicit local scope limits are retained: collision definitions not required
(2.4, printed p.11 [PDF 14]); no nuclide formulas for nuclear energy sources
(2.10.1, printed p.14 [PDF 17]); diverging lenses only mentioned with eye-defect
correction (4.3.3, printed p.25 [PDF 28]). The second does not exclude the nuclide
equations expressly required in Nuclear Physics. Another 35 bullets carry
qualitative depth, simple applications or stated conditions. The 61 practical
bullets are retained as raw requirements, including a malformed pair on printed
p.34 [PDF 37]; they are not 61 clean, independent activity specifications.
