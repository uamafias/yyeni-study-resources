## Papers

Structure/durations: p.19 [PDF 22]; weights: p.20 [PDF 23]. All three papers are compulsory.

| Paper | Duration | Marks | Weight | Structure |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 2h 15m | 80 | 40% | Four compulsory structured questions; 4 × 20 marks; stimulus material. |
| 2 | 2h 00m | 70 | 35% | Two compulsory structured questions; 2 × 35 marks; related source material. |
| 3 | 1h 45m | 50 | 25% | Two compulsory sections: supplied development-issue data and the learner’s own investigation. |

Paper 3 (Alternative to coursework): Section A uses supplied data (35 marks); Section B concerns the learner’s own investigation (15 marks), p.22 [PDF 25]. Learners investigate individually or in groups; each presents their own report. The investigation is not assessed as formal coursework, but Paper 3 questions concern it (p.23 [PDF 26]).
