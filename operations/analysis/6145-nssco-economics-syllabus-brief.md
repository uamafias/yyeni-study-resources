## Content and scope

Printed pages 3–12 (PDF 7–16) specify **nine themes, 28 topics and 96 specific-objective bullets** before decomposition of listed members. Themes: What is Economics?; Nature and functions of organisations and financial institutions; The market; Occupation, income and expenditure of individuals; Production; Role of Government in an economy; Main economic indicators; Developed and developing economies; Conflicts of interest.

Keep explicit scope that differs from a generic economics course: comparative advantage (7.4), economic rent and transfer earnings (4.2), pricing/output policies in perfect competition and monopoly (3.5), and profit maximisation (5.1). Namibia, Information Technology, HIV and AIDS recur in the specified objectives. Social costs explicitly equal private plus external costs (printed page 12).

No explicit content exclusion of the form “not required” or “will not be assessed” was found in the content pages. Positive limits still bind: elementary theory/basic data skills (printed page 2), simple market situations and elasticity calculations (page 6), simple retail-price-index calculation and application of trade advantage (page 10), simple living-standard measures (page 11). The glossary includes additional terms, including income/cross elasticity and marginal quantities; glossary membership alone must not create independent teaching objectives. Resolve their required use against the content and examination evidence at step 3.
