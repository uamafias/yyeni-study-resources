# Grades 4 to 9: source matrix

Draft, 31 October 2026 (Claude, for Vitalis). This is what the repository holds for every grades 4–9 subject. Both framework drafts in this folder build on it. Nothing here is claimed or planned yet.

## Three generations of junior secondary material

| Generation | Grades and exam | Syllabus in the repo | Papers in the repo | Examiner reports |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Old JSC** (three-year phase) | Grades 8–10. JSC national exam at the end of Grade 10 | none | `Question Banks/Namibian Curricula/JSC (Legacy)/`: 13 subjects, 2013–2019 (184 files) | `Question Banks/Namibian Examiner Reports/JSC (Legacy) /` 2013–2019, one combined report a year |
| **B. Junior Secondary Phase, 2016 edition** | Grades 8–9. Semi-external exam (JSSEE) at the end of Grade 9 | `Syllabi/Namibian Curricula/JSC (Legacy)/` (11 PDFs: Grade 8 in 2017, Grade 9 in 2018; the folder name is misleading) | `Question Banks/Namibian Curricula/JSSEE/`: 6 subjects, 2018, 2019, 2023 and 2024 (44 files) | `Question Banks/Namibian Examiner Reports/JSSEE/JSSEE moderators report 2019.pdf` |
| **C. Junior Secondary Phase, current revision** | Grades 8–9. The syllabus states a DNEA-set, regionally marked semi-external exam at the end of Grade 9 for 6 subjects | `Syllabi/Namibian Curricula/JSC (New)/` (19 PDFs: Grade 8 in 2025, Grade 9 in 2026) | none yet: the first Grade 9 exam on it is the end of 2026 | none yet |

- **Authority.** Generation C is what learners are taught now and the authority for everything we write.
- **How B and A are used.** B and A are evidence about format, tariffs, command words and learner errors, but only after each question is mapped to a C objective (see the crosswalk in `JSS-GR8-9-FRAMEWORK.md`).
- **Grade 10 content.** A's Grade 10 content is out of scope wherever C does not teach it.

## Upper primary (grades 4–7)

No external examination: assessment is continuous assessment plus a school examination. The syllabus sets the mix: in SP Mathematics, continuous assessment is 80 % of the promotion mark in Grade 4 and 65 % in Grade 5, with end-of-term examinations not allowed. Each syllabus has its own assessment objectives and specification grid.

## Gaps to fill before the pilots (step 1)

1. **Specimen papers for the revised junior curriculum (C).** These would be DNEA specimen or sample papers for the 2026 semi-external exam. They do exist for Geography at least: its C syllabus (p. 35) says the Grade 8 school exam "will be set using the specimen paper for Grade 9". Search DNEA and NIED with the Harvester.
2. **Mark schemes.** None are published separately for JSSEE or JSC papers. The examiners' and moderators' reports serve as mark schemes, question part by question part, so a missing report year means that year's papers have no answer evidence.
3. **The 2020–2022 JSSEE papers and the moderators' reports for years other than 2019.** These are probably COVID gaps; to confirm.
4. **The senior primary English syllabus**, and confirmation of whether Environmental Studies is still taught.
5. **A dated provenance check of every revision-notes file.** Revision notes are unofficial. They are used only to cross-check coverage, never copied, and never treated as an authority.
