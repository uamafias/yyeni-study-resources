# Cambridge International AS Level Business 9609 Qualification Profile

**Examination years:** 2026, 2027 and 2028  
**Syllabus version:** 2  
**Selected route:** AS Level only  
**Machine-readable companion:** `cambridge_9609_as_2026_2028.yaml`

## Scope

AS Level candidates study topics 1.1–5.5:

- 1 Business and its environment
- 2 Human resource management
- 3 Marketing
- 4 Operations management
- 5 Finance and accounting

A Level topics 6.1–10.4 are excluded from the AS-only core unless explicitly requested as enrichment or future-stage preparation.

## Papers

### Paper 1 — Business Concepts 1

- 1 hour 15 minutes
- 40 marks
- 40% of the AS Level
- Section A: four compulsory questions of five marks each
- Section B: one essay chosen from two, with two parts and 20 marks in total
- The second essay part should demonstrate evaluation

AO weighting in Paper 1:

- AO1: 35%
- AO2: 30%
- AO3: 20%
- AO4: 15%

### Paper 2 — Business Concepts 2

- 1 hour 30 minutes
- 60 marks
- 60% of the AS Level
- Two compulsory data-response questions of 30 marks each
- Each question has six parts and uses written, numerical and/or diagrammatic stimulus
- The final part of each question should demonstrate evaluation

AO weighting in Paper 2:

- AO1: 30%
- AO2: 30%
- AO3: 20%
- AO4: 20%

## Qualification-wide assessment objectives

- **AO1 Knowledge and understanding — 30%**
- **AO2 Application — 30%**
- **AO3 Analysis — 20%**
- **AO4 Evaluation — 20%**

## Planning implications

- Notes must not be designed as definition banks only because 70% of the qualification weighting lies beyond AO1.
- Application must be modelled through meaningful business context.
- Paper 2 resources must prepare learners to select and use written, numerical and diagrammatic evidence.
- Evaluation should be explicitly taught for Paper 1 essay conclusions and final Paper 2 parts.
- The entire AS course is designed around approximately 180 guided learning hours, so topic depth must be proportionate.
- Learners are not assumed to have studied Business before starting the course.
- Examples should be suitable for learners' age, cultural background and learning context.
